## OVERVIEW

**EB1C** is an employment-based first-preference immigrant visa category for **multinational managers and executives** who have been employed abroad for at least 1 year by a qualifying organization and are being transferred to a US office of the same employer (or parent/subsidiary/affiliate).

**Why EB1C is relevant to you:**
- ✅ You work for **Disney Streaming** (multinational company with offices worldwide, including India)
- ✅ You're planning 2-3 years in India (exceeds the 1-year foreign employment requirement)
- ✅ EB1C has **no priority date backlog** for India (unlike EB2 which has 5-10 year wait)
- ✅ Disney has India operations (Disney+ Hotstar, production studios, tech offices)
- ✅ Provides green card faster than EB2 or potentially EB1A

## EB1C ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

### For the Employee (You):

#### 1. **Foreign Employment Requirement** ✅
- Must have worked **abroad** (outside US) for the same employer or qualifying affiliate for **at least 1 continuous year** within the 3 years preceding admission to US
- **Your situation**: 
  - ✅ Currently at Disney Streaming US
  - ✅ Planning 2-3 years in India
  - ✅ After 1 year in India with Disney India → requirement met
  - ⚠️ Must be in **managerial or executive** capacity during India stint

#### 2. **Qualifying Relationship** ✅
- US employer and foreign employer must have qualifying corporate relationship:
  - Parent/subsidiary
  - Branch office
  - Affiliate (common ownership/control)
- **Your situation**:
  - ✅ Disney is global multinational
  - ✅ Disney has India operations:
    - Disney+ Hotstar (streaming service)
    - Disney India (production, content)
    - Disney tech offices in Bengaluru, Mumbai
  - ✅ Qualifying relationship exists

#### 3. **Managerial or Executive Capacity** ⚠️
Must be employed in **managerial or executive** capacity in both:
- Foreign position (India) - during the 1 year abroad
- US position (upon return) - the position you're returning to

**Managerial Capacity** means:
- Manages the organization, department, subdivision, function, or component
- Supervises and controls work of professional employees or manages an essential function
- Has authority to hire/fire or recommend personnel actions
- Exercises discretion over day-to-day operations

**Executive Capacity** means:
- Directs management of organization or major component/function
- Establishes goals and policies
- Exercises wide latitude in discretionary decision-making
- Receives only general supervision from higher-level executives, board, or shareholders

**Your Current Role**: Lead Software Engineer at Disney Streaming
- ⚠️ "Lead Software Engineer" is typically **NOT** managerial/executive (it's a senior technical role)
- ⚠️ Need to assess: Do you manage people? Budget? Strategic decisions?
- ✅ **Solution**: Would need to transition to **Engineering Manager** or **Senior Engineering Manager** role in India

## EB1C PETITION PROCESS

### Step 1: India Employment (1 Year Minimum)
- Disney India entity employs you as Engineering Manager
- Supervise team (3-5+ engineers)
- Clear managerial duties (hiring, performance reviews, strategic decisions)
- Document: Org chart, job description, decision-making authority

### Step 2: EB1C I-140 Petition Filing
**Timing**: After completing 1 year in India, before returning to US

**Petitioner**: Disney US entity

**Required Evidence**:
1. **Qualifying Relationship**:
   - Corporate documents showing Disney US and Disney India relationship
   - Stock ownership, organizational charts
   - Tax returns, financial statements

2. **Foreign Employment**:
   - Employment contract with Disney India
   - Payroll records (1 year minimum)
   - Job description (managerial duties)
   - Organizational chart showing your position
   - Evidence of supervisory authority (performance reviews you conducted, hiring decisions)

3. **US Position**:
   - Job offer letter for US return role (managerial capacity)
   - US organizational chart
   - Description of managerial duties upon return

4. **Your Qualifications**:
   - Resume/CV
   - Educational credentials
   - Work history

**Timeline**: 
- Regular processing: 6-12 months
- Premium processing: 15 business days ($2,805 additional fee)

**Cost**: 
- I-140 filing fee: $715
- Premium processing (optional): $2,805
- Attorney fees: $5,000-10,000 (employer pays)
- **Total**: $5,715-13,520 (Disney's cost)

### Step 3: I-485 (Green Card Application)
**Timing**: After I-140 approval AND returning to US

**Process**:
- File I-485 (Adjustment of Status) for you and family
- Includes EAD (work authorization) and AP (travel document) while pending
- Medical exam, biometrics, interview
- Timeline: 6-18 months

**Cost**: 
- I-485 per person: $1,440 (you, wife, 2 kids) = $5,760
- Medical exams: $200-500 per person = $800-2,000
- Attorney fees: $3,000-5,000
- **Total**: $9,560-12,760 (you pay, not Disney)

## KEY CONSIDERATIONS & GOTCHAS

### 1. **Genuine Managerial Role Required**
USCIS scrutinizes EB1C heavily. The role must be **genuinely** managerial, not:
- ❌ "Lead Engineer" with no reports (senior IC)
- ❌ "Technical Lead" who guides but doesn't supervise
- ❌ "Manager" in title only with no real authority

**What qualifies**:
- ✅ Supervise 3-5+ professional employees (engineers)
- ✅ Authority to hire, fire, promote, set compensation
- ✅ Budget responsibility
- ✅ Strategic decision-making (architecture, roadmap, priorities)
- ✅ Spend <50% time on day-to-day operational tasks

### 2. **Same Employer Requirement**
- Must be **same employer** or qualifying affiliate
- ⚠️ If you resign from Disney and join different Indian company, EB1C path is lost
- ✅ Disney India → Disney US qualifies
- ❌ Disney US → [resign] → Different Indian company → Disney US does NOT qualify

### 3. **Timing is Critical**
- Must complete **1 full year abroad** before filing I-140
- Must still be employed by foreign entity when I-140 is filed
- Must intend to work in managerial capacity in US upon return

### 4. **H1B During Transition**
- ⚠️ If you resign from Disney US (abandon H1B) to join Disney India, you'll need **new H1B** when returning
- ✅ **Better approach**: Disney US "transfers" you to Disney India, then "transfers back" (internal mobility)
- Ask Disney if they can structure it as **secondment** or **international assignment** rather than resignation

### 5. **No Self-Petitioning**
- ⚠️ EB1C requires employer sponsorship (unlike EB1A which you can self-petition)
- Disney must be willing and must file the petition
- You cannot force Disney to sponsor

### 6. **India Operations Reality Check**
- Disney+ Hotstar is major operation in India (millions of subscribers)
- Disney has tech and content teams in India
- BUT: Is there an actual **Engineering Manager opening** for your skill set?
- You'll need to research internal Disney India roles and openings
