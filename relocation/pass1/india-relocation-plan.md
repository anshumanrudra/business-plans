### 1. Immigration & Visa Strategy

#### Green Card Process
**Critical Decision Point:** The timing of your Green Card application is crucial.

**Option A: File Green Card BEFORE leaving**
- File I-140 (employer-sponsored immigrant petition) before departure
- Once I-140 is approved, you can extend H-1B beyond 6 years indefinitely (in 3-year increments)
- **Pros:**
  - Preserves priority date
  - Allows H-1B extensions upon return
  - Faster path to Green Card upon return
- **Cons:**
  - Requires employer support while abroad
  - Must maintain intent to return to same/similar position
- **Action Items:**
  - [ ] Discuss with Disney HR/Immigration team about filing I-140 before departure
  - [ ] Understand Disney's policy on extended international assignments
  - [ ] Explore "leave of absence" vs "remote work from India" arrangements

**Option B: Start fresh after return**
- Restart H-1B and Green Card process upon return
- **Pros:**
  - Clean break, no obligations during India stay
  - Flexibility in job changes
- **Cons:**
  - Lose 2-3 years in Green Card queue (India backlog is 20+ years currently)
  - May exhaust H-1B time limit
  - Children may age out (21+) of derivative Green Card eligibility

**Option C: L-1 Intra-company Transfer**
- Transfer to Disney India office on L-1A (manager) visa equivalent
- Work remotely for Disney from India
- Return on L-1 visa, then transition to Green Card
- **Pros:**
  - Maintains employment continuity
  - Dual intent visa (can apply for Green Card)
  - L-1 to Green Card path exists
- **Cons:**
  - Requires Disney India entity and suitable role
  - May not provide enough flexibility for parent care
- **Action Items:**
  - [x] Check if Disney has operations in Bengaluru
  - [ ] Explore remote engineering roles based in India

#### Children's Status
