### 4. Disney Manager Conversation (Informal)
**Why:** Gauge receptiveness before formal HR process

**Timing:** Schedule 1:1 THIS WEEK (30 min)

**Approach:** Keep it exploratory, not demanding
```
Script:
"[Manager name], I wanted to discuss a personal situation. 
My father in India is going through serious health challenges - 
he's on dialysis 3x/week and my mother is struggling to manage alone.

I'm exploring the possibility of relocating to India for 12-18 months 
to help with his care. I wanted to talk with you before going to HR 
to understand if there are options like:

1. Extended unpaid leave of absence
2. Part-time remote work from India (10-15 hrs/week)

What are your thoughts? What would be the process to explore this?"
```

- ❌ **Negative:** "Company can't support remote work from India" → Plan to resign
