# Relocation Logistics

US → India (Bengaluru or Kolkata) | Family of 4 (43/40, kids 13/7) | 2-3 year stay | Target move within 2-3 months (by ~Sep 2026). Cost-saving prioritized throughout. ~USD/INR figures are approximate; INR at ~83/USD.

---

## 1. Shipping ~20 Medium Boxes US → India

**Compare (before India duty):**

| Option | Total (~20 boxes) | Transit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sea consolidator (per-box) — RECOMMENDED** | **~$1,800-2,100** | 2-4 months | Door-to-door; cheapest reliable |
| Sea, self-handled legs | ~$1,100-1,440 | 2-4 months | You drop off / pick up |
| Air freight | ~$1,500-3,500 | 1-2 weeks | Billed on volume; bulky = costly |
| Excess checked baggage | ~$100-225/bag | with you | Only ~6-12 bags realistic |
| Courier (FedEx/DHL) | ~$3,600-5,400+ | 3-5 days | 2-3x cost; urgent boxes only |

- **Sea is the value play.** US→India personal goods sell *per box*, not per cubic meter. **Universal Relocations** 2026 table: 16-25 boxes = **$100/box** door-to-door (box limit 24x18x18 in, ≤50 lb; +$25 over 50 lb; India port fee ~$13-14/box). **SFL Worldwide**: ocean $2.00/lb (50 lb min), 10 boxes = $895. Both are the viable US-origin consolidators. (Schumacher Cargo does not serve India; ShipToIndia is defunct.)
- **Air freight** bills on volumetric weight (~167 kg/CBM, IATA), so light bulky boxes "volumize" to 3-5x actual weight - bad for boxes. (Maersk/Freightos)
- **Excess baggage** is cheapest *per pound* (~$4-8/kg) and arrives with you - but use it as a *supplement* for 6-12 valuable/urgent bags, not the whole move (see Section 7 for per-airline fees).
- **Courier** = fastest (3-5 days) but 2-3x; reserve for 1-2 time-critical boxes only.

**Ship vs. buy in India (India = 230V/50Hz vs US 120V/60Hz):**
- **SHIP:** Anything reading "INPUT 100-240V, 50/60Hz" - laptops, phone/tablet/camera chargers, shavers (just need a ~$5 plug adapter). Clothing (light, duty-free, sentimental). Rare/professional/signed books only. Non-electric cookware, knives, cast iron, china.
- **DO NOT SHIP (120V-only - will fail or catch fire on 230V):** hair dryers, toasters, kettles, coffee makers, irons, microwaves, blenders, space heaters, mini-fridges. US TVs (ATSC vs India DVB-T2 tuner mismatch, streaming region-locks). Ordinary furniture (low value/cu-ft, monsoon warps wood). Ordinary books. Rebuy these locally.
- **Knives/kitchen cutlery:** fine to ship in a **sea container/box** (used household goods); the only knife restriction is the ordinary aviation rule against blades in airline carry-on/checked baggage. (expat.com; BackToIndia)

**Recommendation:** Sea consolidator (Universal Relocations or SFL) for the ~20 boxes (**~$1,800-2,100, 2-4 months**); add 2x23 kg free bags + a couple of prepaid extra checked bags per person on your flights for valuables. Ship used goods only (see customs timing below). *Note: Bengaluru is landlocked - sea cargo clears at Chennai then trucks inland; budget the extra transit.*

## 2. India Customs — Transfer of Residence (TR)

**IMPORTANT — rules just changed:** The **Baggage Rules, 2026** (CBIC Notification 14/2026-Customs N.T., effective **~1-2 Feb 2026**) superseded the 2016 rules. Structure is the same but INR caps were raised. **Most mover websites still quote the old ₹5 lakh / ₹50,000 figures - they are outdated.** Current figures below. (CBIC; TaxGuru — *single-source on the 2026 figures; verify against the CBIC e-Gazette or a customs broker before relying on them.*)

**Does this family qualify? YES (this is the key answer):**
- Full TR requires **2 years' stay abroad immediately preceding arrival**, AND total short visits to India in those 2 years **≤ 6 months (180 days)**, AND **not having availed TR in the prior 3 years**. (CBIC Baggage Rules 2026)
- **There is NO requirement to intend to stay in India permanently, no minimum-stay-after-arrival, and no clawback if you leave again.** Eligibility is purely backward-looking (your years in the US), so a planned 2-3 year stay does **not** disqualify you.
- Returning NRIs / Indian-passport / OCI / PIO all qualify; foreign nationals on a non-tourist visa also qualify. Only tourist-visa holders are excluded.

**Duty concessions under TR (used personal & household effects):**
- Bona fide **used** household articles clear **duty-free**, with aggregate value caps by stay abroad: 3-12 mo = ₹1.5 lakh; 1+ yr = ₹3 lakh; **2+ years = ₹7.5 lakh (~$9,000)**. (CBIC Notification 14/2026)
- **Plus** a separate general duty-free allowance of **₹75,000** (~$900) for residents/Indian-origin. (CBIC 2026)
- Typical qualifying items: clothing, books (also 0% IGST), furniture, linens, kitchenware, one each of fridge/washer/microwave/AC/laptop/PC per family.
- Gold (2026): now weight-based after >1 yr abroad — **female up to 40 g, others up to 20 g**.

**Non-TR / your ~20 boxes:**
- Genuinely **used personal effects clear duty-free in their own right** (used clothes, printed books, used household items), accompanied or as unaccompanied baggage. (CBIC Rule 3)
- **Unaccompanied baggage timing (Rule 10/2026):** must be in your possession abroad, **dispatched within 1 month of your arrival** in India, and may land **up to 2 months before** arrival. Clear via Red Channel against your passport. *Plan shipping around your arrival date.*
- **New or still-packaged items get NO free buffer on unaccompanied shipments and are dutiable.**

**Duty if NOT under TR / on dutiable goods ≈ 38.5%** (35% Basic Customs Duty + 10% Social Welfare Surcharge) on the baggage rate (post-2026 exact rate uncertain — verify). Ignore the "15%" (concessional appliance rate) figures on mover sites. (CBIC)

**Prohibited/restricted:** narcotics, obscene material, counterfeit goods, wildlife products, drones/satellite phones (without license). Alcohol >2 L heavily dutiable (~150-180%) - don't ship. Flat-panel TVs get **no** TR relief. **Vehicle import carries punitive duty - do NOT bring the Tesla to India.** (CBIC Annexure-I)

**Paperwork:** passport with entry/exit stamps (proving 2 yrs abroad), visa/OCI, **TR declaration + Customs Baggage Declaration**, itemized valued packing list, Bill of Lading/Airway Bill, proof of residence abroad (work letter/lease/utilities), PAN/Aadhaar (consignee KYC). You must have arrived in India before goods clear; use a licensed Customs House Agent under Power of Attorney.

**Recommendation:** You qualify for full TR (₹7.5 lakh duty-free + ₹75,000 allowance). Ship **used goods only**, avoid electronics/TVs/alcohol, dispatch within the Rule-10 window, and carry full paperwork.

## 3. US House — Rent Out vs. Keep Empty

**Recommendation: RENT IT OUT.** Leaving it empty is the costly, risky choice.

- **Empty-home risk:** standard homeowners insurance generally **stops covering a vacant home after 30-60 days**; you'd then need a **vacant-home policy that costs ~50-60% more and often excludes theft/vandalism**. Empty homes also deteriorate undetected (a slow leak becomes major damage) and attract vandals/squatters. (Insurance.com, citing III)
- **Renting** keeps it occupied, maintained, and income-generating against your ~$3.2k/mo payment.
- **Property management (essential while abroad):** **~8-12% of monthly rent (avg ~10%)** plus a leasing/tenant-placement fee of **half to one month's rent**. They handle marketing, screening, rent collection, maintenance, evictions. (Stessa)
- **Landlord insurance:** you **must switch** from homeowners (an HO-3 may deny claims on a rented home; lenders require it). Costs **~25% more** but covers structure, liability, and loss of rental income. (ValuePenguin/Progressive, III)
- **Rental income** in many markets covers most of the ~$3.2k PITI; net against ~10% management, ~25% higher insurance, maintenance, and vacancy gaps.
- **Tax (see finance section §4):** rental income is taxable (Schedule E); residential depreciation over 27.5 yrs; **as a nonresident landlord, US-source rent faces a default 30% gross withholding** unless you file **Form W-8ECI** + make the **§871(d) net election**. **Engage a tax advisor before you leave.** (IRS Pub. 527)

## 4. Tesla Model Y (2024) — 2-3 Year US Storage

**Battery (HV):**
- Tesla's directive: **leave it plugged in** to reliable power - "maintains the Battery at the optimum level of charge." Set charge limit to **~50-60%** (mid-range is most chemically stable). (Tesla Owner's Manual; Recurrent)
- Vampire drain **~1%/day** (Tesla's figure); **Sentry Mode ON drains 7-15%/day - turn it OFF.** Never let it hit 0% (risks pack/12V damage, flatbed-tow recovery). (Tesla; Electrek)
- **2-3 year nuance:** "plugged in and forgotten" is risky (one power outage = slowly depleting); "unplugged" depletes in ~2-3 months. **Only safe setup = plugged into reliable power + ~50-60% limit + a local contact checking every 4-8 weeks.**
- Enable Low Power/vacation mode if on software 2025.32+.

**12V (16V Li-ion):** charged by the HV pack; stays healthy *as long as the HV pack has charge*. If HV hits 0%, the 12V dies and the car goes unresponsive. (Recurrent)

**Tires:** for 2-3 years, **put it on rated jack stands (4,400+ lb) at factory jack points** - eliminates flat-spotting and relieves suspension. If not on stands, roll it every 2-4 weeks. Leave **parking brake OFF** (seizes in humidity); use wheel chocks. (Hagerty; Tesla)

**Shed/indoor storage:** target **30-50% RH** with a **dehumidifier (~45-55%) + ventilation + hygrometer**; seal gaps. Rodent control: seal entry points, dryer sheets/traps, pop the hood, **no poison**. Breathable custom-fit cover (never plastic/tarp). (Hagerty; Sidev)

**Insurance — READ FIRST:** **Because there's a lienholder, you must keep full coverage (comprehensive + collision) the whole time, even parked** - you generally cannot drop to comp-only until the loan is paid. Do NOT cancel (triggers expensive force-placed insurance + a lapse). Lower cost via higher deductibles + a "stored/non-driving" rate; add **gap coverage** (new EV depreciates fast). (III; Progressive)

**Registration/tolls/software:** file **Planned Non-Operation (PNO)** so it's legal off-road (~$28/yr in CA - check your state); re-file annually. Tesla EVs are smog-exempt. Close/return the **FasTrak/E-ZPass** tag and refund the balance. Keep the car on a **live Wi-Fi network** for free OTA updates; let **Premium Connectivity ($9.99/mo) lapse**.

**0% loan:** **Do NOT pay it off early** (no interest to save). **Never miss a payment** (a 30-day late can void the 0% promo and tanks excellent credit). Set up **lender-side ACH autopay from a funded US checking account** with a multi-payment buffer; confirm online access works from abroad. (Bankrate; Experian; CFPB)

**Caretaker (every 4-6 weeks):** confirm still plugged in / SoC ~50-60% via the app; short 15-30 min drive (incl. highway) to scrub brake rotors and exercise the 12V (skip if on stands but still check SoC); check tires; inspect for rodents/leaks; check the dehumidifier.

## 5. The 24'x15' (360 sq ft) Shed

- **Build cost (slab + electrical):** basic wired shed **~$12,000-22,000**; finished detached garage **~$25,000-40,000** (~$60-90/sq ft). Slab $2,000-7,000; wiring $1,000-2,500. (Bob Vila; Fixr; HomeAdvisor)
- **Permit:** required (anything >100 sq ft); **~$100-1,500** depending on jurisdiction. (Fixr)
- **Insulation/climate control: NOT required** for dead car storage - an uninsulated, weather-tight, ventilated slab structure is fine (add a dehumidifier for the car per Section 4).
- **Cost-justified? NO, over 2-3 years.** Indoor/vehicle storage runs **~$130-200/mo** ($75-225 for dedicated car storage), i.e. **~$3,600-7,200 over 3 years** - far below the $12k-40k to build. **Build only if you want the structure permanently** (a custom garage adds resale value; rented storage doesn't). (Move.org; Neighbor.com; Insurify)
- **Insurance:** the shed is covered under homeowners "Other Structures" (~10% of dwelling limit); **the car inside is NOT covered by homeowners** - it needs its own comprehensive auto coverage. (III; Policygenius)

**Recommendation:** For a 2-3 year stay, **rent indoor storage (~$150-200/mo) instead of building** - saves ~$8k-35k. Build the shed only if you'll keep it long-term for home value/utility.

## 6. US-Side Affairs to Maintain While Abroad

- **Mail:** USPS temporary forwarding maxes at **12 months** - too short. Best: a **virtual mailbox (~$10-30/mo: PostScan Mail / Anytime Mailbox)** giving a real US address + on-demand scanning, or a trusted **family member's address** (free, but no scanning; watch state-tax nexus). (USPS; PostScan)
- **Banking:** keep accounts open with a US address (foreign address can trigger restrictions). Foreign-IP logins often get blocked - a US-based VPN is the common workaround; keep a US number for 2FA. Avoid **dormancy** (12-24 months inactive) with a small login + transfer every few months.
- **Credit cards:** keep your oldest no-fee cards open; put a small recurring charge (a streaming sub) on **autopay** so they're not closed for inactivity. Use a **0%-foreign-transaction-fee card** for any India spend.
- **US phone number (keep one - banks/IRS often text OTPs only to US numbers):** cheapest reliable option is a real-SIM line - **Tello or US Mobile ~$5-10/mo**. Google Voice ($20 one-time port, do it *before* leaving) works but some banks reject VoIP for 2FA.
- **Driver's license:** renewal abroad is state-dependent (many allow online/mail with a US address, but block it when a photo/vision test is due - check your DMV). Get a **AAA International Driving Permit ($20, valid 1 yr, India recognizes it)** to drive in India; consider a local Indian license for a multi-year stay.
- **Subscriptions:** keep one cheap streaming sub + cloud/password manager + phone + virtual mailbox (autopay anchors); **pause** gym/home internet (vacation holds); cancel geo-locked services.

## 7. Flights — Family of 4 + Extra Baggage

- **One-way is NOT cheaper** for US-India (often equal or more). Round-trips average ~$968; cheap economy fares $455-691; cheapest months Jan-Feb and Sep-Oct, peaks June and December. (KAYAK) **Price both; buy one-way only if it's cheaper or close** (the return leg is a throwaway for a multi-year move).
- **Routes:** Air India flies the only US nonstops (ORD/EWR/JFK/SFO → DEL/BOM; **no US nonstop to Bengaluru**). Best economy value + India-city reach is **1-stop via the Gulf**: **Qatar (DOH) serves BLR directly** (best for Bengaluru/Phase 1); Emirates (DXB) and Etihad (AUH) comparable. For Kolkata, Gulf carriers connect well.
- **Baggage (critical for a baggage-heavy move):** Gulf carriers and **Air India "CLASSIC" fare and up give 2x23 kg free per person** = **8 free 50-lb bags for the family**; Air India's cheapest "VALUE" fare = only 1 bag; **United gives only 1 free bag** (avoid for this move). Extra bags: Emirates **$225**, Qatar ~$200, Etihad $300, **buy them online in advance (up to ~50-65% cheaper than airport).** (UpgradedPoints)
- **Save:** book 3-6 months ahead (off-peak), avoid June-mid-Aug/December/Diwali (+30-45%); Tue/Wed departures ~10-20% cheaper; compare *total* cost including bag fees. Set Google Flights alerts. No pet noted - skip pet logistics.
- **Synergy with Section 1:** the 8 free + ~4-8 paid extra bags ($100-225 each) become your supplemental "shipping channel" for valuables arriving with you.

## 8. Prioritized 8-12 Week Relocation Timeline

**Weeks 1-2 — Decisions & big-ticket bookings**
- Lock the move date (target after Sept 30, 2026 for tax + cheaper fares). Phase-1 city = Bengaluru; line up India housing/schools for the 13- and 7-year-old.
- **Book flights now** (Gulf carrier with 2x23 kg free bags; price one-way vs round-trip).
- Engage a **property manager** for the US house; start landlord-insurance switch and a tax advisor for nonresident withholding.
- Decide shed vs. rented storage (recommend **rented storage** for 2-3 yrs).

**Weeks 3-4 — Customs & shipping setup**
- Confirm **TR eligibility** (2 yrs abroad, ≤6 mo India visits, no TR in 3 yrs); gather passports with stamps, visas, residence proof.
- Get quotes from **Universal Relocations / SFL**; build the **itemized valued packing list**. Plan dispatch within the Rule-10 window (within 1 month of arrival).
- Begin **purge/sort**: ship (used clothes, books, sentimental cookware) vs. store-in-US vs. donate/sell. Confirm what NOT to ship (120V appliances, TVs, furniture).

**Weeks 5-6 — Tesla & US-affairs**
- **Tesla:** set ~50-60% charge limit + plugged in, Sentry OFF, jack stands, dehumidifier in storage space; set up **lender ACH autopay**; file **PNO**; switch to stored/low-use full-coverage policy (keep comp+collision - lien); close FasTrak; let Premium Connectivity lapse; brief the caretaker.
- **US affairs:** set up virtual mailbox / family address; port/keep a US number; set credit-card autopay; confirm bank access from abroad; get AAA IDP; check DL renewal.

**Weeks 7-8 — Execute the move**
- **Pack and hand off the ~20 boxes to the sea consolidator** (timed to dispatch within 1 month of your India arrival).
- Move remaining US belongings to storage/shed; park and finalize the Tesla.
- Finalize tenant for the house (or keep secured if not yet rented); transfer/forward utilities.
- Prep travel docs; pack the 8 free + paid extra checked bags with valuables/essentials.

**Weeks 9-12 — Fly & clear customs (buffer)**
- **Fly to India.** On/after arrival, clear unaccompanied baggage via Red Channel with passport + TR declaration + packing list (use a Customs House Agent).
- Settle in: India SIM, local bank account, school enrollment, register address. Consider a local Indian driver's license.
- Confirm from abroad: rent flowing, Tesla autopay + insurance active, caretaker doing 4-6 week checks, US mail/banking accessible.

---

**Top cost-savers:** (1) Sea consolidator + free/extra airline bags instead of courier/air (~$1,800 vs $3,600-5,400). (2) Rent storage instead of building a shed (saves ~$8k-35k). (3) Rent the house, not leave it empty. (4) Don't prepay the 0% Tesla loan. (5) Use TR for ~₹7.5 lakh duty-free; ship used goods only.

**Verify before relying on numbers:** 2026 TR INR caps (CBIC e-Gazette / customs broker), your state's DL/PNO rules and your loan's exact 0%-promo terms, live fares for your specific cities/dates, and exact bank dormancy windows.
