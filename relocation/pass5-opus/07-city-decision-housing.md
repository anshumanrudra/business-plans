## 4. The Key Trade-Off

**The crux:** Your parents are physically in Bengaluru *right now* (father's 3x/week dialysis at Narayana Bommasandra), but they intend to return to their owned home in Nadia, WB once father's AV fistula matures. **AV fistula maturation typically takes 6–12 weeks** (often up to 3-4 months before it's reliably usable for needling — longer given his diabetes/age) — so the parents' Bengaluru stay is plausibly a **3–6 month window**, not years.

- **Base in Bengaluru immediately:** maximizes value *during the fistula window* — you're beside your parents for the most medically fragile period, beside your sibling, with the best schools and your strongest local job market. **But** the moment parents return to Nadia, your primary caregiving reason evaporates and you're 1,500+ km from them — and Kolkata/Nadia is where they'll spend the bulk of your 2-3 year stay.

- **Base in Kolkata immediately:** positions you for the long-term WB phase + cultural fit + lower cost. **But** you're absent during the parents' most vulnerable Bengaluru months, far from your sibling, and the kids would face a school move if you later concluded Bengaluru was better — wasted churn.

**The decisive insight:** these are not symmetric bets. The Bengaluru need is *acute but short* (fistula window); the Kolkata need is *sustained* (most of the 2-3 years, once parents go home). A single permanent choice forces you to sacrifice one entirely. A **phased approach captures both** — and the timing lines up cleanly with the school calendar and the fistula maturation clock.

## 5. Final Recommendation + Housing Checklist

### RECOMMENDATION: PHASED — Start in Bengaluru, then relocate to Kolkata (New Town) when parents return to Nadia.

**Phase 1 (now → parents' WB move, ~3-6 months):** Base in **Bengaluru, Off-Sarjapur Rd / Choodasandra** (or Electronic City for max cost-saving + dialysis proximity). You cover the father's critical fistula-maturation period beside him and your sibling, the kids enroll at a Sarjapur-belt school, and your husband has the strongest job/remote setup. Choose a **school whose curriculum (IGCSE/CBSE) transfers cleanly to Kolkata** (DPS network or Cambridge boards exist in both cities) to minimize disruption at the move.

**Phase 2 (after parents resume dialysis in WB):** Relocate to **Kolkata, New Town** — closest to Kalyani/Nadia (Kalyani ~48km, AIIMS Kalyani + JNM for father's backup care), lower cost (saves meaningfully toward the husband's savings goal), cultural fit, and good schools (DPS Newtown). This is where you spend the majority of the 2-3 years.

**Why phased over committing to one:** It is the only option that doesn't abandon the parents during the high-risk dialysis-access period *and* doesn't leave you stranded 1,500km away once they're home in WB. The fistula timeline (weeks, not years) makes Phase 1 short and the disruption tolerable. **If you must pick ONE city** (to avoid two moves with kids 13 & 7): choose **Kolkata/New Town** — it aligns with where parents will actually live for most of your stay, your savings goal, cultural fit, and is far cheaper; accept Bengaluru visits during the fistula window.

### Housing Action Checklist (next 2-3 months)

1. **Confirm father's fistula maturation date** with the Narayana Bommasandra nephrologist — this sets the Phase 1 length and the whole timeline.
2. **Confirm Nadia/Kalyani dialysis slot in advance** — phone AIIMS Kalyani / JNM Hospital / Krishnanagar Eskag unit to reserve a maintenance-HD slot before parents move; register for **Swasthya Sathi** (makes dialysis effectively free).
3. **Bengaluru: lock a short/flexible lease** (6-11 month, negotiate break clause) in Off-Sarjapur/Choodasandra or Electronic City via NoBroker/MagicBricks/99acres. Verify ACT or JioFiber fiber at the address before signing (remote-work critical).
4. **School: apply now to a transferable-curriculum school** — prioritize boards present in both cities (DPS/CBSE or Cambridge IGCSE). Get a "transfer/withdrawal" understanding in writing to ease the Phase 2 move.
5. **Scout Kolkata New Town remotely** — shortlist DPS Newtown / DPS Megacity / Newtown School; pre-identify 3BHK gated complexes (₹25k–45k); confirm Alliance/Wishnet/Jio fiber.
6. **Budget:** Bengaluru Phase 1 ~₹70k-90k/mo all-in (Sarjapur) or ~₹50k-60k (E-City); Kolkata Phase 2 ~₹45k-60k/mo all-in — **the move to Kolkata is your main savings lever** (rent, groceries, help all cheaper).
7. **Avoid premium/long Bengaluru commitments** (HSR/Bellandur villas, 3-year leases, heavy furnishing) — you are deliberately temporary there.
