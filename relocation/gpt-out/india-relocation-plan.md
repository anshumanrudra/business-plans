# Relocation to India (2–3 Month Execution Plan)

## Primary Objectives
1. Relocate to Bengaluru within 2–3 months.
2. Support aging parents, especially father's dialysis needs.
3. Avoid disruption in children's education.
4. Enable spouse to work in India.
5. Preserve future U.S. immigration optionality.

---

# Recommended Strategy

## Phase 1 (Week 1–2): Make the Go/No-Go Decision

### Employment
- Confirm internal transfer possibility with Disney (India role, remote role, contractor arrangement, or resignation).
- If no transfer is available, begin India job search immediately.
- Preserve all U.S. immigration documents (I-140 approval, H-1B approvals, paystubs, tax returns).

### Finances
- Create a relocation budget.
- Maintain U.S. bank accounts.
- Keep 6–12 months of expenses in liquid savings.

### House
Choose one:
- Rent the house through a property manager (preferred).
- Sell only if long-term India stay is certain.

### Car
- Sell Tesla before departure.
- Clear remaining auto loan.
