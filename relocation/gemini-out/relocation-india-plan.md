## Phase 1: The Immediate 30-Day Critical Path

### 1. Immigration & Status Preservation (Protecting the Return Option)

Since your goal includes returning to the US for your children's higher education, preserving your hard-earned priority date is paramount.

* **H-1B & I-140:** Your approved I-140 with a May 2021 priority date is safe. Even if you leave your current employer, that priority date is yours for life (unless revoked for fraud). You can use it to port to a new employer if/when you return to the US.
* **OCI Cards:** If your children are US citizens, initiate their **Overseas Citizenship of India (OCI)** applications immediately. If time runs short, get them **Entry Visas (X-Visas)** so they can travel, and complete the OCI process at the FRRO in Bengaluru.
* **Wife's Work Status:** As an OCI cardholder (or Indian citizen), your wife will have full authorization to work in India immediately without needing a separate work visa.

### 2. Education Continuity (Zero-Lapse Strategy)

Moving mid-academic year or short-notice means prioritizing school admission in Bengaluru *now*.

* **School Selection:** Target **International Baccalaureate (IB)** or **IGCSE** schools in Bengaluru. These curricula match the US system much better than CBSE/ICSE, making both the transition into India and the eventual transition back to US colleges seamless.
* **Location Constraints:** Look for schools in or near Sarjapur, Electronic City, or Kasavanahalli. This keeps your kids close to your parents and sibling (Choodasandra/Bommanasandra corridor), minimizing school bus commute times.
* **Action:** Contact admissions teams *this week* for mid-term or immediate enrollment options. Request official, sealed transcripts and immunization records from West District Elementary and your older son’s school.

### 3. Healthcare Handover

Your mother is currently carrying a heavy burden. Relieving her is a top priority.

* **Narayana Health (Bommanasandra):** Because your father is already established here, contact his nephrologist immediately. Confirm that his current dialysis slot (3x a week) is stable and inquire about transferring medical records or coordinating with a local home-health nursing agency for post-dialysis care.

### 1. Your Career & EB-1A Runway

Your background in computer science and lead-level streaming/backend architecture makes you highly competitive in Bengaluru’s tech hubs.

* **Internal Transfer:** Your quickest path is to ask Disney Streaming if they can transition you to a remote role via their India entity (Disney Star / Hotstar) based in Bengaluru.
* **Building the EB-1A Profile:** Returning to India is actually an excellent opportunity to accelerate an EB-1A (Alien of Extraordinary Ability) profile, as it doesn't require an employer sponsor and bypasses the backlogs if approved. While in India, focus on three specific criteria:
1. **Judging the Work of Others:** Volunteer to judge major international hackathons, review papers for IEEE/ACM conferences, or serve on panels for tech startup accelerators.
2. **Scholarly Articles:** Author technical whitepapers or deep-dives into data pipeline engineering and stream processing for major tech journals or high-impact industry mediums.
3. **Critical Role:** Ensure your next position in India (whether with Disney or a new tech firm) is designated as a "Critical or Essential Role" for an organization with a distinguished reputation.
