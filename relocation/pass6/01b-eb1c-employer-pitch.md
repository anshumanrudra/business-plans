# EB-1C Employer Pitch & Managerial Role Package

*pass6 addendum. Practical, copy-editable templates to secure the **written managerial job description + org placement** that anchors an EB-1C petition. Two variants: **Disney India (internal transfer — first choice)** and **InfoServices India (external move — backup)**. Have your immigration attorney review the final role description before it's issued on letterhead — the wording is what an adjudicator reads.*

---

## 0. What You're Actually Asking For (and why the wording matters)

You are **not** asking HR for "a green card." You're asking for a **manager-level role abroad** that you'd want anyway — and making sure it's *documented* in EB-1C-defensible language. The petition later stands on paper that already exists: a role description, an org chart, and appraisals. Get the paper right up front and the year abroad does the rest.

**The three things every document must reinforce (INA 101(a)(44)(A) — personnel management):**
1. You **manage people** — a team of professional/supervisory employees (target **≥4–6 direct reports**).
2. You have **authority** — hire/fire/promote input, performance appraisals, and budget/headcount say.
3. You operate at a **managerial altitude** — you direct the work; you do **<50%** hands-on individual-contributor engineering.

Avoid language that makes you sound like a senior IC: "hands-on architect," "individual technical contributor," "codes daily." Prefer: "leads," "directs," "manages the team responsible for," "sets priorities and objectives for," "responsible for hiring, performance, and development of."

---

## 1. Diligence Questions to Resolve BEFORE You Commit (either employer)

Send these to the attorney + the sponsoring HR/leadership. If any answer is weak, that's your RFE risk:

- [ ] **Qualifying relationship:** Is the US entity the parent / subsidiary / affiliate / branch of the India entity? What documents prove it (share certificates, ownership chart, annual report, board overlap)?
- [ ] **US "doing business ≥1 year":** Does the US entity have active, ongoing US operations (payroll, clients, office) — not just a shell? For at least the prior year?
- [ ] **The India role:** Will it have **real direct reports from day one** (how many, what levels)? Not a "team of one" or a paper team.
- [ ] **Authority:** Will you actually own hiring, appraisals, and a budget/headcount plan?
- [ ] **The US return role:** Will the US entity place you in a **managerial/executive** role on return (required for EB-1C), not back to pure IC?
- [ ] **Premium Processing** available for EB-1C (E13) at filing? (Confirm current fee ~$2,805.)
- [ ] **L-1A** feasibility as the return vehicle (blanket L for a large firm like Disney; individual L-1A otherwise).

## 2. VARIANT A — Disney India (Internal Transfer, First Choice)

### 2A. Initial email to your Disney manager / HR business partner

> **Subject: Exploring a manager-level transfer to a Disney India engineering team (2–3 yr family relocation)**
>
> Hi [Manager/HRBP name],
>
> For family reasons — my parents in India need long-term caregiving — I'm planning a 2–3 year relocation to India and would very much like to **stay with Disney** through it rather than step away.
>
> I'd like to explore transferring to a **manager-level engineering role at [Disney India / Disney+ Hotstar / the Bengaluru technology org]**, leading a team there, on **local India payroll**. I'm currently a Lead Software Engineer on [team]; I see this as a natural step up into people leadership, and it would let me keep contributing to Disney while I'm abroad.
>
> Could we set up time to discuss (a) whether a suitable engineering-manager opening exists in Bengaluru, (b) the internal-transfer / assignment process, and (c) how payroll and reporting would work? I'd also want to loop in immigration counsel on the paperwork so it's clean on both ends.
>
> Timeline: I'm targeting a move after Sept 2026. Happy to be flexible to fit an org need.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

### 2B. What to request in writing once there's interest
1. A **written offer/role letter** for the India manager role (see the template in §4) on Disney India letterhead.
2. Confirmation of **local India payroll / assignment structure** (this is your "employment abroad" — unpaid leave does NOT count for EB-1C).
3. An **org chart** placing you as manager over the named team.
4. Confirmation the **US entity will place you in a managerial role on return** and support an **L-1A** + EB-1C filing.

### 2C. Why Disney is the low-risk sponsor
Disney US ↔ Disney India is a textbook qualifying relationship, the US entity obviously "does business," and a large org easily justifies "needs a manager." The managerial-capacity prong is where small petitioners get denied — Disney sidesteps most of that. **If Disney India can offer a genuine people-manager seat, this is the clean answer** and it aligns with your goal of staying at Disney.

## 3. VARIANT B — InfoServices India (External Move, Backup)

*Use only if Disney India can't offer a real managerial seat. This means **leaving Disney** and joining InfoServices' India entity as a manager for 1+ year. Higher small-petitioner scrutiny — so the documentation must be airtight.*

### 3A. Initial email to InfoServices leadership / HR

> **Subject: Returning to InfoServices in a manager role at the India entity (EB-1C-supported)**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm relocating to India for 2–3 years and exploring rejoining **InfoServices at [India entity name]** in a **manager-level engineering/delivery role**, leading a team locally. As you know, InfoServices holds my approved I-140 (PD May 2021), and I'd want to build toward an **EB-1C (multinational manager)** green card sponsored by InfoServices US, using my time managing the India team.
>
> For that to work cleanly, the role would need to be **genuinely managerial from the start** — a team of [4–6+] engineers reporting to me, with real hiring/appraisal/budget responsibility. Could we discuss whether such a role exists (or can be structured), the India-payroll setup, and whether InfoServices US would sponsor the EB-1C and support an L-1A on my return?
>
> I'd want our respective immigration counsel to align on the corporate-relationship documentation early.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

### 3B. Extra diligence specific to InfoServices (the RFE-bait items)
- [ ] **Prove the US↔India ownership link** on paper (this is *the* prong USCIS probes for smaller firms). If it's loose/informal, the petition is at risk.
- [ ] Confirm the **US entity is genuinely operating** (staffing/consulting firms sometimes have thin US operations — a denial risk).
- [ ] Ensure the India team is **real headcount**, not assembled on paper for the petition.
- [ ] Confirm InfoServices will place you in a **US managerial role on return**.

## 4. Managerial Role Description Template (both variants — the load-bearing document)

*Have this issued on India-entity letterhead, signed by the hiring executive. Attorney to finalize wording.*

---

**Position:** Engineering Manager / Senior Engineering Manager — [Team/Function], [Company] India
**Location:** [Bengaluru]  **Employment:** Full-time, local India payroll  **Reports to:** [Director/VP title]

**Position summary:**
[Name] leads and manages the [team/function] responsible for [product/domain]. The role is a **managerial position**: the incumbent directs the team's work, sets objectives and priorities, and is responsible for the hiring, performance management, and professional development of the team. The incumbent does not perform the day-to-day individual technical work of the team; that work is carried out by the professional staff who report to this position.

**Direct reports:** [4–6+] professional employees, including [e.g., Senior Software Engineers, Software Engineers, QA Engineer, and Team Lead], as shown on the attached organization chart.

**Managerial duties (with approximate time allocation — must total majority managerial):**
- Directs and manages the [team] and its deliverables — ~25%
- Sets team objectives, priorities, roadmaps, and resource allocation — ~20%
- **Hires, interviews, and makes hiring/promotion/termination decisions** for the team — ~15%
- **Conducts performance appraisals** and manages development/PIPs for direct reports — ~15%
- Owns the team **budget / headcount plan** and vendor/tooling spend — ~10%
- Represents the team to senior leadership and cross-functional stakeholders — ~10%
- High-level technical direction/architecture *oversight* (not hands-on coding) — ~5%
- **Individual hands-on engineering: <5%** *(keep this line minimal — do not describe daily coding)*

**Authority:** Full authority over hiring, performance ratings, promotions, and team budget within [Company] India policy. Discretion over team structure and work assignment.

---

### Attach to the role description:
- **Organization chart** — a box for you (titled Manager), your manager above, and your [4–6+] reports below, each with name + title.
- Confirmation of **India payroll** (offer letter / assignment letter).

## 5. Ongoing Evidence to Generate During the Year (so the petition writes itself)

Keep a running folder from month 1:
- [ ] **Signed offer/role letter** + org chart (from §4).
- [ ] **Performance appraisals you author** for each direct report (at least one cycle).
- [ ] **Hiring artifacts** — interview feedback you wrote, offer approvals you signed, requisitions you opened.
- [ ] **Budget/headcount plans** you owned or approved.
- [ ] **Team communications** showing you directing work (meeting notes, planning docs, OKRs you set).
- [ ] **Payroll records** proving continuous employment for the full 1 year in the managerial role (no gaps).
- [ ] Periodically updated **org chart** as the team evolves.

*This is the difference between "I had a manager title" and "here is documented proof I managed people for a year." USCIS credits the latter.*

---

## 6. Sequence

1. **Now (Phase 0):** send the §2A / §3A email to your chosen sponsor; run the §1 diligence with counsel; get the §4 role description + org chart *in writing before you move*.
2. **Move → work abroad (Phase 1–2):** start managing on day one; generate §5 evidence every month.
3. **~1-year mark:** US entity files **EB-1C I-140** (ports the 2021 PD; $715 + PP ~$2,805).
4. **Return (Phase 3):** re-enter on **L-1A** (manager, dual-intent) or cap-exempt H-1B + recapture; finish the GC via consular processing (Mumbai) / AOS.

> **Reminder:** keep the **EB-1A file building in parallel** and **O-1A in reserve** — all carry the same 2021 PD (see `01a-eb1c-plan.md`). The employer pitch here is the primary track, not the only one.
