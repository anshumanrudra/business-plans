# Relocation Logistics

*pass6 best-of synthesis. US → India (Bengaluru first, then Kolkata) | Family of 4 (43/40, kids 13/7) | 2-3 year stay | Target move within 2-3 months (target departure after ~Sep 30, 2026 for tax). Cost-saving prioritized throughout. USD/INR figures approximate; INR at ~83/USD.*

---

## 1. Shipping ~20 Medium Boxes US → India

**Compare (before India duty):**

| Option | Total (~20 boxes) | Transit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sea consolidator (per-box) — RECOMMENDED** | **~$1,800-2,100** | 2-4 months | Door-to-door; cheapest reliable |
| Sea, self-handled legs | ~$1,100-1,440 | 2-4 months | You drop off / pick up |
| Air freight | ~$1,500-3,500 | 1-2 weeks | Billed on volume; bulky = costly |
| Excess checked baggage | ~$100-225/bag | with you | Only ~6-12 bags realistic |
| Courier (FedEx/DHL) | ~$3,600-5,400+ | 3-5 days | 2-3x cost; urgent boxes only |

- **Sea is the value play.** US→India personal goods sell *per box*, not per cubic meter.
  - **Universal Relocations** 2026 table: 16-25 boxes = **$100/box** door-to-door (box limit 24x18x18 in, ≤50 lb; +$25 over 50 lb; India port fee ~$13-14/box).
  - **SFL Worldwide**: ocean $2.00/lb (50 lb min), 10 boxes = $895.
  - Both are viable US-origin consolidators. (Schumacher Cargo does not serve India; ShipToIndia is defunct.)
  - **Full-service alternatives to quote against** (for comparison / if you want more hand-holding): **Crown Relocations** (US→India specialist), **AGS Movers**, **Allied International**, **Suddath**. Get 2-3 quotes.
- **Self-pack the boxes** (don't buy the packing service) to **save ~$500-1,000**.
- **Air freight** bills on volumetric weight (~167 kg/CBM) — light bulky boxes "volumize" to 3-5x actual weight, bad for boxes.
- **Excess baggage** is cheapest *per pound* (~$4-8/kg) and arrives with you — use it as a *supplement* for 6-12 valuable/urgent bags, not the whole move.

**Ship vs. buy in India (India = 230V/50Hz vs US 120V/60Hz):**
- **SHIP:** Anything reading "INPUT 100-240V, 50/60Hz" — laptops, phone/tablet/camera chargers, shavers (just need a ~$5 plug adapter). Clothing (light, duty-free, sentimental). Rare/professional/signed books only. Non-electric cookware, knives, cast iron, china.
- **DO NOT SHIP (120V-only — will fail/catch fire on 230V):** hair dryers, toasters, kettles, coffee makers, irons, microwaves, blenders, space heaters, mini-fridges. US TVs (tuner + streaming region mismatch). Ordinary furniture (low value/cu-ft, monsoon warps wood). Ordinary books. Rebuy locally.
- **Knives/kitchen cutlery:** fine to ship in a sea box (used household goods); the only knife restriction is the aviation rule against blades in airline baggage.

**Recommendation:** Sea consolidator (Universal Relocations or SFL) for the ~20 boxes (**~$1,800-2,100, 2-4 months**); self-pack; add 2×23 kg free bags + a couple of prepaid extra checked bags per person on your flights for valuables. Ship used goods only. *Note: Bengaluru is landlocked — sea cargo clears at Chennai then trucks inland; budget the extra transit.*

## 2. India Customs — Transfer of Residence (TR)

**IMPORTANT — rules just changed:** The **Baggage Rules, 2026** (CBIC Notification 14/2026-Customs N.T., effective **~1-2 Feb 2026**) superseded the 2016 rules. Structure is the same but INR caps were raised. **Most mover websites still quote the old ₹5 lakh / ₹50,000 figures — they are outdated.** *(CBIC; TaxGuru — single-source on the 2026 figures; **verify against the CBIC e-Gazette or a customs broker** before relying on them.)*

**Does this family qualify? YES:**
- Full TR requires **2 years' stay abroad immediately preceding arrival**, AND total short visits to India in those 2 years **≤ 6 months (180 days)**, AND **not having availed TR in the prior 3 years**.
- **There is NO requirement to intend to stay in India permanently, no minimum-stay-after-arrival, and no clawback if you leave again.** Eligibility is purely backward-looking (your years in the US), so a planned 2-3 year stay does **not** disqualify you.
- Returning NRIs / Indian-passport / OCI / PIO all qualify; only tourist-visa holders are excluded.

**Duty concessions under TR (used personal & household effects):**
- Bona fide **used** household articles clear **duty-free**, with aggregate value caps by stay abroad: 3-12 mo = ₹1.5 lakh; 1+ yr = ₹3 lakh; **2+ years = ₹7.5 lakh (~$9,000)**.
- **Plus** a separate general duty-free allowance of **₹75,000** (~$900).
- Typical qualifying items: clothing, books (also 0% IGST), furniture, linens, kitchenware, one each of fridge/washer/microwave/AC/laptop/PC per family.
- Gold (2026): weight-based after >1 yr abroad — **female up to 40 g, others up to 20 g**.

**Your ~20 boxes (non-TR path also works):**
- Genuinely **used personal effects clear duty-free in their own right**, accompanied or as unaccompanied baggage.
- **Unaccompanied baggage timing (Rule 10/2026):** must be in your possession abroad, **dispatched within 1 month of your arrival**, may land **up to 2 months before** arrival. Clear via Red Channel against your passport.
- **New/still-packaged items get NO free buffer on unaccompanied shipments and are dutiable.**

**Duty if NOT under TR / on dutiable goods ≈ 38.5%** (35% Basic Customs Duty + 10% Social Welfare Surcharge) — post-2026 exact rate uncertain, verify. Ignore "15%" figures on mover sites.

**Prohibited/restricted:** narcotics, obscene material, counterfeit goods, wildlife products, drones/satellite phones (without license). Alcohol >2 L heavily dutiable (~150-180%). Flat-panel TVs get **no** TR relief. **Vehicle import carries punitive duty — do NOT bring the Tesla to India** (unanimous across all research).

**Paperwork:** passport with entry/exit stamps (proving 2 yrs abroad), visa/OCI, **TR declaration + Customs Baggage Declaration**, itemized valued packing list, Bill of Lading/Airway Bill, proof of residence abroad, PAN/Aadhaar (consignee KYC). Arrive in India before goods clear; use a licensed Customs House Agent under Power of Attorney.

## 3. US House — Rent Out (don't leave empty)

**Recommendation: RENT IT OUT.** Leaving it empty is the costly, risky choice.

- **Empty-home risk:** standard homeowners insurance generally **stops covering a vacant home after 30-60 days**; a **vacant-home policy costs ~50-60% more and often excludes theft/vandalism**. Empty homes deteriorate undetected and attract vandals/squatters.
- **Property management (essential while abroad):** **~8-12% of monthly rent (avg ~10%)** plus a leasing/tenant-placement fee of **half to one month's rent**.
- **Landlord insurance:** you **must switch** from homeowners (lenders require it). Costs **~25% more** but covers structure, liability, and loss of rental income.
- **Rental income** in many markets covers most of the ~$3.2k PITI — plausibly a **net surplus of ~$700-1,200/mo** after management/insurance/maintenance in typical markets.
- **Tax (see Finance §4):** rental income is taxable (Schedule E); depreciation over 27.5 yrs; **as a nonresident landlord, US-source rent faces a default 30% gross withholding** unless you file **Form W-8ECI** + make the **§871(d) net election**. **Engage a tax advisor before you leave.**

## 4. Tesla Model Y (2024) — 2-3 Year US Storage

*(Decision confirmed: keep and store. Do not sell, do not ship to India, do not prepay the 0% loan — see Finance §6. Earlier drafts that said "sell" assume a permanent move and are wrong for a 2-3 yr return.)*

**Battery (HV):**
- Tesla's directive: **leave it plugged in** to reliable power. Set charge limit to **~50-60%** (mid-range is most chemically stable).
- Vampire drain **~1%/day**; **Sentry Mode ON drains 7-15%/day — turn it OFF.** Never let it hit 0%.
- **2-3 year setup = plugged into reliable power + ~50-60% limit + a local contact checking every 4-8 weeks.** ("Plugged in and forgotten" risks a power outage slowly depleting it; unplugged depletes in ~2-3 months.) Do **not** disconnect the 12V (contrary to some earlier advice) — the HV pack keeps the 12V healthy as long as it has charge.
- Enable Low Power/vacation mode if on software 2025.32+.

**Tires:** for 2-3 years, put it on **rated jack stands (4,400+ lb) at factory jack points** — eliminates flat-spotting. **Inflate to max sidewall PSI** before lifting (further reduces flat-spotting). If not on stands, roll it every 2-4 weeks. Leave **parking brake OFF** (seizes in humidity); use wheel chocks.

**Storage environment:** target **30-50% RH** with a **dehumidifier + ventilation + hygrometer**; seal gaps. Rodent control: seal entry points, dryer sheets/traps, pop the hood, **no poison**. Breathable custom-fit cover (never plastic/tarp).

**Insurance — READ FIRST:** **Because there's a lienholder, keep full coverage (comprehensive + collision) the whole time, even parked** — you generally cannot drop to comp-only until the loan is paid, and canceling triggers expensive force-placed insurance + a lapse. Lower cost via higher deductibles + a "stored/non-driving" rate; add **gap coverage**.

**Registration/tolls/software:** file **Planned Non-Operation (PNO)** (~$28/yr in CA — check your state; re-file annually); Tesla EVs are smog-exempt. Close/refund the **FasTrak/E-ZPass** tag. Keep the car on **live Wi-Fi** for free OTA; let **Premium Connectivity ($9.99/mo) lapse**.

**0% loan:** **Do NOT pay it off early** (no interest to save). **Never miss a payment** (a 30-day late can void the 0% promo). Set up **lender-side ACH autopay from a funded US checking account** with a multi-payment buffer; confirm online access works from abroad.

**Caretaker (every 4-6 weeks):** confirm still plugged in / SoC ~50-60% via the app; short 15-30 min drive (skip if on stands but still check SoC); check tires; inspect for rodents/leaks; check dehumidifier. **Compensate ~$50-75/visit**; give an explicit rule: **"charge to 55% if SoC ever drops below 40%."**

**Where to store:** rented indoor car storage runs **~$130-250/mo** — named options to price: **Public Storage, StorQuest, Luxury Auto Storage CT**.

## 5. The 24'x15' (360 sq ft) Shed — Don't Build It

- **Build cost:** basic wired shed **~$12,000-22,000**; finished detached garage **~$25,000-40,000** ($60-90/sq ft). Slab $2,000-7,000; wiring $1,000-2,500. Permit **~$100-1,500**.
- **Cost-justified over 2-3 years? NO.** Rented indoor/vehicle storage runs **~$130-200/mo = ~$3,600-7,200 over 3 years** — far below $12k-40k. **Build only if you want the structure permanently** (a custom garage adds resale value; rented storage doesn't).
- **Insurance:** the shed is covered under homeowners "Other Structures"; **the car inside is NOT covered by homeowners** — it needs its own comprehensive auto coverage.

**Recommendation:** For a 2-3 year stay, **rent indoor storage (~$150-200/mo) — saves ~$8k-35k.** Use a separate general storage unit (~$100-200/mo) for household belongings you're not shipping, if needed.

## 6. US-Side Affairs to Maintain While Abroad

- **Mail:** USPS temporary forwarding maxes at **12 months** — too short. Use a **virtual mailbox (~$10-30/mo: PostScan Mail / Anytime Mailbox)** for a real US address + on-demand scanning, or a trusted family member's address (watch state-tax nexus).
- **Banking:** keep accounts open with a US address (foreign address can trigger restrictions). Consider **Charles Schwab** checking (no FX fees, worldwide ATM rebates, expat-friendly) as your maintained US account. Foreign-IP logins often get blocked — a US-based VPN + a US number for 2FA is the common workaround. Avoid **dormancy** (small login + transfer every few months).
- **Credit cards:** keep your oldest no-fee cards open; put a small recurring charge on **autopay**. Use a **0%-foreign-transaction-fee card** for India spend.
- **US phone number** (banks/IRS often text OTPs only to US numbers): cheapest reliable = a real-SIM line — **Tello or US Mobile ~$5-10/mo**. Google Voice ($20 one-time port, do it *before* leaving) works but some banks reject VoIP for 2FA.
- **Money transfer:** use **Wise** for US→India (mid-market rate + ~0.4-0.6% fee) — far cheaper than bank wires. Route converted funds to **NRE** (see Finance §7).
- **Driver's license:** renewal abroad is state-dependent. Get a **AAA International Driving Permit ($20, valid 1 yr, India recognizes it)**; consider a local Indian license for a multi-year stay.
- **Subscriptions:** keep one cheap streaming sub + cloud/password manager + phone + virtual mailbox (autopay anchors); **pause** gym/home internet; cancel geo-locked services.

## 7. Flights — Family of 4 + Extra Baggage

- **One-way is NOT necessarily cheaper** for US-India. Round-trips average ~$968; cheap fares $455-691; cheapest months Jan-Feb and Sep-Oct; peaks June/December. **Price both; buy one-way only if it's cheaper or close.**
- **Routes:** Air India flies the only US nonstops (to DEL/BOM; **no US nonstop to Bengaluru**). Best economy value + India-city reach is **1-stop via the Gulf**: **Qatar (DOH) serves BLR directly** (best for Bengaluru/Phase 1); Emirates (DXB) and Etihad (AUH) comparable.
- **Baggage (critical for a baggage-heavy move):** Gulf carriers and **Air India "CLASSIC" fare and up give 2×23 kg free per person** = **8 free 50-lb bags for the family**; Air India's cheapest "VALUE" fare = 1 bag; **United gives only 1 free bag** (avoid). Extra bags: Emirates **$225**, Qatar ~$200, Etihad $300 — **buy online in advance (up to ~50-65% cheaper).**
- **Save:** book 3-6 months ahead (off-peak); avoid June-mid-Aug/December/Diwali (+30-45%); Tue/Wed departures ~10-20% cheaper; compare *total* cost including bag fees.
- **Synergy with §1:** the 8 free + ~4-8 paid extra bags become your supplemental "shipping channel" for valuables arriving with you.

## 8. Prioritized 8-12 Week Relocation Timeline

**Weeks 1-2 — Decisions & big-ticket bookings**
- Lock the move date (target after Sept 30, 2026 for tax + cheaper fares). Phase-1 city = Bengaluru; line up India housing/schools.
- **Book flights** (Gulf carrier with 2×23 kg free bags; price one-way vs round-trip).
- Engage a **property manager** for the US house; start landlord-insurance switch and a cross-border tax advisor for nonresident withholding.
- Decide shed vs. rented storage (recommend **rented storage**).

**Weeks 3-4 — Customs & shipping setup**
- Confirm **TR eligibility**; gather passports with stamps, visas, residence proof.
- Get quotes from **Universal Relocations / SFL** (+ Crown/AGS/Allied/Suddath for comparison); build the **itemized valued packing list**; self-pack; plan dispatch within the Rule-10 window.
- Begin **purge/sort**: ship vs. store-in-US vs. donate/sell.

**Weeks 5-6 — Tesla & US-affairs**
- **Tesla:** ~50-60% charge + plugged in, Sentry OFF, jack stands (max PSI), dehumidifier; set **lender ACH autopay**; file **PNO**; switch to stored/low-use full-coverage policy; close FasTrak; let Premium Connectivity lapse; brief the caretaker (SLA: $50-75/visit, charge to 55% if below 40%).
- **US affairs:** virtual mailbox / family address; port/keep a US number; credit-card autopay; confirm bank access from abroad (open Schwab if useful); get AAA IDP; check DL renewal.

**Weeks 7-8 — Execute the move**
- **Pack and hand off the ~20 boxes to the sea consolidator** (timed to dispatch within 1 month of your India arrival).
- Move remaining US belongings to storage; park and finalize the Tesla.
- Finalize tenant for the house; transfer/forward utilities.
- Pack the 8 free + paid extra checked bags with valuables/essentials.

**Weeks 9-12 — Fly & clear customs (buffer)**
- **Fly to India.** Clear unaccompanied baggage via Red Channel with passport + TR declaration + packing list (use a CHA).
- **Bridge housing:** stay with the sibling in Choodasandra (or a serviced apartment ~₹50-80k the first month) while apartment-hunting — avoids signing a lease sight-unseen.
- Settle in: India SIM, local bank account, school enrollment, register address. Consider a local Indian driver's license.
- Confirm from abroad: rent flowing, Tesla autopay + insurance active, caretaker doing 4-6 week checks, US mail/banking accessible.

---

**Top cost-savers:** (1) Sea consolidator + self-pack + free/extra airline bags (~$1,800 vs $3,600-5,400). (2) Rent storage instead of building a shed (saves ~$8k-35k). (3) Rent the house, not leave it empty. (4) Don't prepay the 0% Tesla loan. (5) Use TR for ~₹7.5 lakh duty-free; ship used goods only.

**Verify before relying on numbers:** 2026 TR INR caps (CBIC e-Gazette / customs broker), your state's DL/PNO rules and your loan's exact 0%-promo terms, live fares for your specific cities/dates, and bank dormancy windows.
