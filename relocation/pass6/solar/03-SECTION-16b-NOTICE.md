I am the Lessee under the SunPower Financing Agreement, Lease #4593311, dated January 16, 2023 and countersigned
January 19, 2023, for the solar photovoltaic system installed at 498 Plainville Ave, Unionville (Town of

Section 16(b) provides that SunPower Capital, LLC ("You," "We," or "Lessor") will be in default under the Lease
if it fails to perform a material obligation under the Lease and "do not initiate a remedy of such failure
within a period of ninety (90) days" after receiving notice of such failure. This letter is that notice. It
identifies three material obligations that Lessor has failed to perform, and it starts the ninety (90) day
period running from the date this notice is received.

## Failure 2 — Production Guarantee (Guarantee Period 1)

Exhibit A, Section 2(c) and Table A set a Production Guarantee for Guarantee Period 1 of 10,465 kWh at a
Guaranteed Energy Price of $0.187 per kWh. Guarantee Period 1 ended on or about May 2025. To date, no Payment
Amount reflecting any shortfall between the Production Guarantee and the Actual Biennial kWh generated by the
System has been remitted to me, and no biennial reconciliation has been provided.

I demand that Lessor perform the biennial reconciliation required by Exhibit A for Guarantee Period 1 and
provide the supporting production data (Actual Biennial kWh, as measured by the SunPower Monitoring System)
used to calculate it, together with any Payment Amount due.

This is a failure to perform the material Production Guarantee obligation of Exhibit A, Section 2(c).

## Failure 3 — Real property and refinance cooperation

Lease Section 5(c)(xiv) obliges Lessor not to place a lien on my Home or Property. Exhibit E (Fannie Mae
B2-3-04 Compliance) represents that Lessor does not have a lien on the Real Property, that Lessor will not
impede any sale of the Real Property, and that Lessor's interest is subject and subordinate in all respects to
the Security Instrument held by my lender.

A filing appears of record against the Property that I believe implicates these obligations. [FACTS — see
01-EVIDENCE-CHECKLIST.md E2 land records / UCC search]. I demand (a) written confirmation that any such filing
does not encumber the Real Property, consistent with Exhibit E, and (b) a subordination agreement or estoppel
letter, in recordable form, confirming that Lessor's interest is subject and subordinate to my lender's Security
Instrument and does not impede any sale or refinancing of the Property.

This is a failure to perform the material real-property and refinance-cooperation obligations of Section
5(c)(xiv) and Exhibit E.

## Demand

I demand that Lessor initiate a remedy of each of the three failures described above within ninety (90) days
of receipt of this notice, as required by Section 16(b). If Lessor does "not initiate a remedy of such failure
within a period of ninety (90) days" after receipt of this notice, Lessor will be in default under Section
16(b), and I intend to exercise the purchase option made available under Section 11(a)(iv) "in the event of
Our default pursuant to Section 16(b)."
