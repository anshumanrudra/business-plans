NOT LEGAL ADVICE — SELF-PREPARED DRAFT. Verify all research items in the spec §14 before filing.

**Purpose.** The operational runbook for getting `04-JAMS-DEMAND.md` filed, served, and heard — what goes in
the package, where it goes, what it costs, how it is served, who else gets a copy, how the hearing is requested

## 2. Where to file

**Provider and rules.** JAMS, administered under its Streamlined Arbitration Rules (claims under $250,000). Rule
13 requires the parties to "conclude the document and information exchange process within fourteen (14)
calendar days after all pleadings or notices of claims have been received" — see `05-DOCUMENT-REQUESTS.md` for
the requests this clock governs. Rule 17(g) governs remote hearings — see §6 below.

**Venue.** Hartford County, Connecticut — the county nearest Claimant's Home, consistent with Lease §19's
direction that "the arbitration shall be held in the location that is most convenient to your Home." Lease §19's
fallback clause, quoted verbatim: "If a JAMS office does not exist in the county where you live, then we will
use another accredited arbitration provider with offices close to your Home." As of this writing JAMS maintains
Connecticut case administration and the fallback is not expected to be triggered; it is recorded here only so
that, if JAMS declines the matter for any reason, the Lease itself supplies the next step rather than requiring
a fresh dispute over forum.

**Standing decision: do not litigate the forum.** Lease §19 commits both parties to JAMS/Streamlined Rules
arbitration, and the forum is not worth contesting — every count in this case is stronger argued to a neutral
arbitrator under Rule 13's fast document exchange than it would be in a motion practice fight over venue or
provider. No motion to compel a different forum, no request for a different provider, and no venue objection
is filed absent a JAMS refusal to administer.

## 3. Fees

- Under Lease §19: "you will be required to pay the first $125 of any filing fee." SunPower ("We") pays "any
  filing fees in excess of $125" and "all of the arbitration fees and costs."
- **Alternative ceiling.** JAMS Consumer Arbitration Minimum Standard 7 independently caps the consumer's
  arbitration fee: "the only fee required to be paid by the consumer is $250." Lease §19's $125 is lower than
  this ceiling, so §19 controls and $125 is the amount actually due at filing. The Standard 7 cap is recorded as
  a fallback in case JAMS's own fee schedule or case-management fee ever conflicts with the Lease figure — the
  consumer fee cannot exceed $250 either way.
- **All remaining fees are on Respondents** — JAMS Filing Fee balance, Case Management Fee, and all professional
  fees for the arbitrator's services, per both Lease §19 and Standard 7.

## 4. Service

**Methods — Lease §21.** Notices under the Lease are given "by personal delivery, electronic mail, overnight
courier, or certified, or registered mail, return receipt requested," and are deemed received on personal
delivery, on acknowledgment of an electronic transmission, on the promised delivery date after deposit with an
overnight courier, or five days after deposit in the mail.

**Practice for this case: certified mail AND email, always.** Every notice and filing that must be served on
SunPower is sent by both certified mail, return receipt requested, and email, regardless of which single method
Lease §21 would technically permit. This gives two independent proof-of-service records and forecloses a dispute
over receipt.

## 5. Statutory copies

Conn. Gen. Stat. § 42-110g(c) provides for a copy of any CUTPA complaint to the Attorney General and the
Commissioner of Consumer Protection when the claim is filed **in court**. This arbitration is not a court action,
so § 42-110g(c) does not, by its terms, require these copies here. Nonetheless, as a matter of practice, a copy
of the JAMS Demand (Count 6, the CUTPA count) is sent to:

- The Connecticut Attorney General's Office, Consumer Protection.
- The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection, Office of the Commissioner.

These copies are **prophylactic, not jurisdictional** — they create a regulatory record of the dispute and cost
nothing, but neither their sending nor their omission affects the arbitration itself. Do not represent to
SunPower or the arbitrator that § 42-110g(c) compels these copies in an arbitration; it does not.

## 6. Remote hearing request

At the preliminary conference (or as early as the Demand permits), request that the Hearing be conducted
remotely under JAMS Streamlined Rule 17(g): "The Arbitrator has full authority to determine that the Hearing, or
any portion thereof, be conducted in person or virtually by conference call, videoconference or using other
communications technology with participants in one or more geographical places, or in a combined form." Ground
the request in Claimant's residence in India and the absence of any prejudice to SunPower, a national company
accustomed to remote proceedings.

## 8. KEEP PAYING — standing instruction

**Do not stop making Monthly Payments at any point in this process, for any reason.** This instruction is
absolute and applies regardless of how strong any count in this case appears.

- Lease §16(a)(i): a payment default occurs, and the thirty-day cure clock starts, the moment any Monthly
  Payment is missed.
- Lease §18 (Early Termination Liability): if SunPower terminates for a payment default, it is entitled to
  recover the unpaid Monthly Payments for the year of default, plus the System's Fair Market Value, plus removal
  costs — an outcome that would erase every objective in this case (lease cancellation, lien removal, and
  keeping the System) in one stroke.
- Exhibit D (ACH Authorization Agreement): terminating the ACH authorization does not terminate the Lease or the
  payment obligation — it only changes the mechanism of payment, not whether payment is owed.

**Payments continue under protest and without prejudice** to every claim, defence, and demand made in this case.
Continued payment is not, and must never be represented as, an acknowledgment that SunPower has performed its
own obligations under the Lease. See the identical instruction and its citations in `03-SECTION-16b-NOTICE.md`
(Reservation of rights) and the "Do not" list in `00-CASE-STRATEGY.md`.
