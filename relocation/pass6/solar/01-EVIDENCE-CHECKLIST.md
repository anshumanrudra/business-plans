# Evidence Checklist — Rudra v. SunPower Capital, LLC

NOT LEGAL ADVICE — SELF-PREPARED DRAFT. Verify all research items in the spec §14 before filing.

**Purpose.** This is the working checklist for spec §8's evidence items (E1–E12 from the spec, plus E13–E14 added
2026-09-23). Every later deliverable (`04` JAMS Demand, `05` Document Requests, `06` Damages Schedule) has blanks
that only this document fills. Each item below states what to request, who to send it to, ready-to-send wording,
what the item proves, which count(s) from spec §6 it serves, and a status line to fill in as requests go out and
documents come back.

**Priority order for actually sending these:** E1 (DocuSign certificate), E2 (fixture filing / UCC search), E3
(refinance file), E4 (deed and title search), then the rest in the order below.

## The date blocker is cleared — interconnection is MAY 2023, not April 2023

**Superseded 2026-09-23.** This section previously read "E7 — the Eversource interconnection approval date —
currently rests on Claimant's recollection only" and directed that no other document state a date as fact until
E7 came back in writing. **Two documents now in hand answer it** (E13, E14 below):

- **2023-05-12** — SAVKAT (Sheri Barracliff, Project Concierge) to Claimant: "Great news! The utility has given
  you permission to operate your system," with instructions to flip the AC disconnect.
- **2023-05-15** — SunPower Customer Service to Claimant, SAVKAT accounting and SunPower's Salesforce archive:
  "YOUR SYSTEM IS ON!"

**What this fixes, and what it does not.**

| Date | Status now |
|---|---|
| Permission to operate granted | **On or before 2023-05-12** — documented (E14) |
| System energized / In-Service Date | **2023-05-12 to 2023-05-15** — documented (E14) |
| Lease Term Start Date (Lease §2 = "the date upon which your utility approves interconnection") | **Bounded at on or before 2023-05-12.** The exact Eversource approval date is still not in hand — E7 remains open **for that one purpose only** |

**The April 2023 interconnection date is withdrawn.** It was recollection, it was never documented, and it is now
contradicted: the system could not have been operating under a permission relayed on 12 May 2023 if approval had
issued in April and energization followed in May as previously assumed. Every deliverable that keyed a clock or a
payment year to "April 2023" has been corrected to May 2023. The consequence is that the Lease Term Start Date and
the In-Service Date now fall **within days of each other in May 2023**, not a month apart — see
`08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`, where the §11(a)(i) and §11(b) clocks now resolve together.

**The residual E7 gap is narrow but real.** Eversource may have approved interconnection days or weeks before
SAVKAT relayed it on 12 May 2023. April 2023 approval with a 12 May relay cannot be excluded on these two
documents alone. Send the E7 request anyway — but the deliverables no longer *wait* on it, because the payment
schedule is now anchored independently by SunPower's own written billing rule (E14; see E5 below).

## E2 — Land records and UCC search

**Request:** certified copies of any fixture filing, UCC-1, notice of lease, or other instrument recorded against
the Property or indexed against Claimant.

**From:** Town of Farmington land records; Connecticut Secretary of State UCC search, run against both Claimant
and SunPower Capital, LLC as secured party.

**Wording to send:**

> I request certified copies of any UCC-1 financing statement, fixture filing, notice of lease, or other
> instrument recorded against 498 Plainville Ave, Unionville/Farmington, CT 06085, or indexed against Anshuman
> Rudra, in which SunPower Capital, LLC, SunPower Corporation, SunStrong, or SAVKAT, Inc. appears as secured
> party or claimant. Please also confirm the recording date, volume and page, and whether the instrument is
> indexed against the real property or against personal property only.

**What it proves:** exactly what was filed and against what — the Real Property or only the System as personal
property. Count 3 is unprovable without this: the breach theory turns on whether the filing as made encumbers
the Real Property, which §5(c)(xiv) and Exhibit E forbid.

**E13 now supplies the seller's side of this, and it is the single most valuable document added 2026-09-23.** On the
Eversource *Third Party Ownership Customer Disclosure*, executed 2023-02-23 over James Ferrughelli's signature for
SAVKAT, the question

> "Will a filing be recorded in the land records of the customer's municipality pursuant to the contract for this
> system?"

is answered **Yes**. That is a contemporaneous representation, made to a state regulator under an express
misrepresentation penalty, that the intended filing was **in the municipal land records** — the real-property
index — and not merely a UCC-1 against the System as personal property. Lease § 5(c)(xiv) and Exhibit E forbid
exactly that.

**Do not overstate it.** The certification proves the *plan*, not the *execution*: it does not establish what was
actually recorded, in which index, or in what scope. **E2 is still required** and Count 3 still stands on the filing
as made. What the certification does is (a) supply the intent behind the filing, (b) make it far harder for
SunPower to characterise any land-records filing as an administrative slip, and (c) give Count 3 a documented
foundation even if the E2 search comes back ambiguous. Read the answer from the rendered page image, not the text
layer — see the checkbox caveat in `sources/SOURCES.md`.

**Serves:** Count 3 (the no-lien covenant breach — now with the seller's own statement of intent). Also supports
Count 6 (CUTPA, if the filing itself is an unfair or deceptive act) — but note the 2023-02-23 certification
**predates the CUTPA boundary** (`04` Count 6 pleads acts on or after 2023-09-21) and so cannot itself be a pleaded
CUTPA act; only a filing made or maintained on or after that boundary can be.

|---|---|---|---|---|
