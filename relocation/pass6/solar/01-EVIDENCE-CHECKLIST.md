# Evidence Checklist — Rudra v. SunPower Capital, LLC

NOT LEGAL ADVICE — SELF-PREPARED DRAFT. Verify all research items in the spec §14 before filing.

**Purpose.** This is the working checklist for spec §8's twelve evidence items. Every later deliverable (`04`
JAMS Demand, `05` Document Requests, `06` Damages Schedule) has blanks that only this document fills. Each item
below states what to request, who to send it to, ready-to-send wording, what the item proves, which count(s)
from spec §6 it serves, and a status line to fill in as requests go out and documents come back.

**Priority order for actually sending these:** E1 (DocuSign certificate), E2 (fixture filing / UCC search), E3
(refinance file), E4 (deed and title search), then the rest in the order below.

---

## Obtain E7 in writing before relying on any date in this case

**E7 — the Eversource interconnection approval date — currently rests on Claimant's recollection only:**
interconnection approved ~April 2023, system energized ~May 2023. No document has yet confirmed either date.

This is not a minor gap. The Lease Term Start Date (Lease §2) is defined by interconnection approval, and every
other date in the case is computed from it: the Production Guarantee Periods (Count 5), the §11(a)(i) purchase
option date (Count 7, currently modeled as opening April 2028), every payment-year figure (Yr1–Yr4 payment
amounts and the running total of payments made), and the boundary of what counts as an "Initial Communication
Date" for guarantee purposes. If the recollected dates are off by even a month, the Guarantee Period 1 true-up
date, the purchase-option date, and the payment-year breakdown all move with it.

**Send the E7 request first, and confirm the written answer before any other document in this set states a date
as fact.**

## E4 — Deed and full title search

**Note before requesting: this item is obtained for the file and is never pleaded.** Per spec §6.2, the fact that
title to the Property is held by more than one owner is retained as evidence and as a prepared defence, not as
an affirmative allegation. This checklist entry states only what to gather. It does not raise, and this document
does not make, any claim or argument about a co-owner's rights, and does not reach any conclusion about the
effect of the filing on any owner who did not sign the Lease.

**Request:** a certified copy of the deed of record, and a full title search or abstract of the Property.

**From:** Town of Farmington land records; a title company or abstractor for the full search.

**Wording to send:**

> I request a certified copy of the current deed of record for 498 Plainville Ave, Unionville/Farmington, CT
> 06085, showing all record owners and the date, volume, and page of recording. I also request a full title
> search or 40-year abstract for the property, showing the complete chain of title, the current form of
> ownership under which title is held (e.g., joint tenancy, tenancy in common, tenancy by the entirety), and
> every mortgage, lien, UCC filing, easement, or other encumbrance currently of record.

**What it proves:** the record ownership of the Property, the form of tenancy under which title is held (which
matters to how any filing against the Property could be treated), and — by comparison with E2 — what SunPower's
own §8(a)(i) real-estate due diligence would have shown had it been performed. It also documents, as a plain
fact for the file, that title is held by more than one owner and what the failed-refinance title examiner
actually flagged.

**Serves: no pleaded count. Obtained for the file and for the reserved §6.2 defence only — not listed, not
exchanged under JAMS Rule 13, and not offered at the hearing.** This item is deliberately *not* tied to Count 3.
Evidence labelled as serving a pleaded count gets listed, exchanged and offered, and the deed and abstract disclose
on their face facts that §6.2 reserves. Count 3's causation is carried by E2 (the filing as made) and E3 (the title
exception in the lender's file), neither of which requires the deed. If a title commitment must be produced for any
reason, redact ownership before producing it.

## E6 — PVS6 monitoring export

**Request:** the complete production history export from the System's monitoring hardware for the entire period
in service.

**From:** SunStrong monitoring portal; repeat as a Rule 13 document request.

**Wording to send:**

> Please provide the complete production history export from the PVS6 monitoring system for the System
> installed at 498 Plainville Ave, Unionville, CT 06085, for the entire period the System has been in service,
> showing kWh produced by month and by Production Guarantee period. Please also provide any internet
> connectivity or communication logs maintained for this system, and any Production Guarantee true-up
> calculation SunPower or SunStrong has performed to date.

**What it proves:** the actual metered kWh production for Guarantee Period 1, and therefore **whether a shortfall
against Table A's 10,465 kWh threshold exists at all.** Do not assume one. The estimated two-year production for
this System is approximately **11,642 kWh** against a **10,465 kWh** guarantee — the guarantee is 90% of the
estimate — so on the estimates the System over-produced and **no Payment Amount is owed**. This export is what
establishes which way it went. If it shows Actual Biennial kWh at or above 10,465 kWh, D4 is $0 and Count 5's money
limb falls away; Count 5's primary limb — the failure to perform the biennial reconciliation and to provide the
production data at all — is unaffected either way.

**Serves:** Count 5 (breach of the Production Guarantee — the reconciliation limb regardless of the figure, and the
Payment Amount limb only if the export shows a shortfall) and Count 6 Route two act (c), which is pleaded as the
failure to *reconcile*, not the failure to remit.

## E10 — The 2023-01-16 proposal and sales communications

**Request:** the sales proposal presented at signing and all texts/emails exchanged with the SAVKAT
representative concerning the sale.

**From:** Claimant's own phone and email records (compile first); also send to SAVKAT, Inc. / SunStrong to
obtain their copy of the proposal, and repeat as a Rule 13 document request.

**Wording to send:**

> Please provide a complete copy of the sales proposal presented to me on or about January 16, 2023, together with
> any pricing worksheet accompanying it, and all text messages and emails exchanged between me and your
> representative concerning the sale.

**Deliberately neutral wording — do not add to it.** This request goes out, pre-Demand, to the party best placed to
warn SunPower and to curate its own file. It therefore asks for the proposal, the pricing worksheet and the message
thread and nothing else. It must not ask about "any statements made about my right to cancel," and must not
reference a discount or a time-limited offer: the first telegraphs Count 1's notice-defect theory before the Demand
is filed, and the second frames a sales-presentation theory that is time-barred and expressly not pleaded. The same
phrasing is kept out of `05-DOCUMENT-REQUESTS.md` R4 and R5 for the same reasons.

**What it proves:** that the sale was personally solicited away from SunPower's place of business, and the manner
and place in which the documents were presented — going to the § 42-134a(a) elements only.

**Serves:** Count 1 (home solicitation sale elements). Not offered on any theory about the content of the sales
presentation.
