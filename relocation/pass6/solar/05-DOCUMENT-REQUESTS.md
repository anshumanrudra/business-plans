# Document Requests — Rudra v. SunPower Capital, LLC

NOT LEGAL ADVICE — SELF-PREPARED DRAFT. Verify all research items in the spec §14 before filing.

**Purpose.** Twenty numbered document requests, R1–R20, addressed to Respondent SunPower Capital, LLC (and, as
noted, to SunStrong as current servicer and SAVKAT, Inc. as soliciting dealer where relevant).

**Baseline.** JAMS Streamlined Rules Rule 13 already requires the parties to cooperate in sharing all
non-privileged documents and information — including electronically stored information — within **fourteen
calendar days** after the claims are received. R1–R20 below are not a discovery motion; they are a statement of
what that existing Rule 13 cooperation obligation already requires Respondent to produce, itemized so nothing is
overlooked and so the fourteen-day clock has a fixed, unambiguous list to run against. Each request below names
the count(s) it serves.

---

### R1 — Complete DocuSign envelope

Produce the complete DocuSign envelope, Envelope ID 3A20E4A8-02E4-4424-A69E-69C610492E40, in native format,
including all documents, fields, and recipient routing as they existed at each stage of the signing process.

**Serves:** Count 1.

### R2 — DocuSign Certificate of Completion and full audit trail

Produce the Certificate of Completion and the full audit trail for the envelope in R1, including every sent,
viewed, and signed timestamp for every recipient, and the signer's IP address and authentication method.

**Serves:** Count 1.

### R3 — Every version of the document set sent

Produce every version of the lease and disclosure document set transmitted to Claimant before, during, and
after the signing process in R1, including any draft, revised, or corrected version and any cover email or
message accompanying each transmission.

**Serves:** Count 1.

### R4 — Sales training materials and scripts

presented this lease to Claimant, and any specification or style guide governing the type size and typeface in
which that disclosure was printed.

**Serves:** Count 1.

### R5 — SAVKAT dealer agreement

around January 2023, including all provisions allocating between SunPower Capital, LLC and SAVKAT, Inc.

Sales Act, Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 42-134a to 42-141, and all provisions concerning which cancellation-rights

**Serves:** Count 1.

### R6 — Every UCC-1, UCC-3, fixture filing, or recorded instrument touching the Property

### R7 — Filing instructions

**Serves:** Count 5, Count 6.

### R17 — Service tickets and dispatch records

**Serves:** Count 4.

### R18 — Policy directing customers to third-party providers at their own expense

Produce any policy, procedure, script, or internal guidance directing customers to obtain repair, maintenance,
or troubleshooting service from third-party providers at the customer's own expense, and any document showing
whether or how that policy was applied to Claimant's account.

**Serves:** Count 4, Count 6.

### R19 — Appraisal or fair-market-value determination for the System

Produce any appraisal, valuation, or fair-market-value determination performed for the System, whether prepared
for insurance, financing, sale, tax, or any other purpose.

**Serves:** Count 7.

### R20 — Federal tax credits claimed and recapture exposure

Produce any document concerning federal tax credits claimed with respect to the System (including any
investment tax credit claimed by SunPower Capital, LLC or an affiliate as owner/lessor), the amount of each such
credit, and any document concerning recapture exposure tied to Lease §11(b), which provides that "If the System is
purchased prior to the fifth (5th) anniversary of the In-Service Date, the Fair Market Value will include the
recapture of any federal tax credits." Produce in particular: the dollar amount of any federal tax credit that
SunPower contends would be recaptured on a purchase of the System; the calculation by which that amount is
determined; and the In-Service Date SunPower uses for the purpose of the §11(b) fifth-anniversary condition.

Claimant makes no assumption that any schedule, step-down, or proration applies: §11(b) states a single
condition — purchase before the fifth anniversary of the In-Service Date — and this request asks for the amount and
its calculation, not for a schedule the Lease does not contain.

**Serves:** Count 7.

## Preservation demand

Claimant demands that SunPower Capital, LLC, SunStrong, and SAVKAT, Inc. preserve, and not delete, overwrite,
or allow to be purged in the ordinary course, all electronically stored information responsive to R1–R20,
including without limitation:

- The complete DocuSign envelope identified in R1, in native format, together with all underlying system logs
  and audit-trail data referenced in R2;
- All monitoring telemetry generated by or derived from the PVS6 monitoring system for the System, including
  raw interval data, aggregated production figures, and any system logs recording changes to monitoring
  configuration or data retention; and
- All communications, in any format including email, SMS, and internal messaging platforms, responsive to any
  request above.

This preservation demand is effective immediately upon receipt and continues until the conclusion of this
arbitration and any appeal.
