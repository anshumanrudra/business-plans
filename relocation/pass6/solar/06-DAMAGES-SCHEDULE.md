**Revised 2026-09-23 — the April-vs-May, 41-vs-42 scenario pair is withdrawn.** This section previously carried a
Scenario A (payment years from an April 2023 interconnection, 42 payments, $3,836.88) as the primary case and a

**Which date governs, and why it is no longer contested.** Two documents now in hand (E14) settle it:

- **2023-05-12** — SAVKAT to Claimant: "the utility has given you permission to operate your system."
- **2023-05-15** — SunPower to Claimant: "YOUR SYSTEM IS ON!" and **"Your payments will be due on the first day of
  the month beginning the month after your system is interconnected."**

Lease § 4(a)'s "one-month anniversary of the Lease Term Start Date" lands in the same month from a Lease Term Start

the exact Eversource approval date.

## D2 — Refinance damages (Count 3)

**Confidence label: ILLUSTRATIVE — REPLACE WITH E3 FIGURES.** No figure in this section is a real number; every
dollar amount below is a placeholder illustration of the formula, not a claim.

**Formula:**

```
D2 = (recast rate − refinance rate quoted) × principal × remaining term
     + recast fees
     + any charges arising from SunPower's §4(b)(i) cooperation failure (e.g., re-application fees,
       rate-lock extension fees, appraisal re-orders)
```

**Worked illustration** — `ILLUSTRATIVE — REPLACE WITH E3 FIGURES`:

| Input | Illustrative value |
|---|---|
| Principal | $400,000 |
| Rate differential (recast rate − refinance rate quoted) | 1.00 point |
| Remaining term | 27 years |
| Resulting differential cost | ~$4,000/yr before discounting |

**This illustration must be replaced** with the actual rate lock, the actual recast rate offered in its place,
the actual remaining term, and any recast/re-application fees, all taken from E3 (the refinance file from the
lender).

**Pleading posture.** Lease §15(a) bars consequential damages, and SunPower will argue a rate differential is
exactly that — consequential, remote, arising from a third party's (the lender's) decision rather than from
SunPower's conduct. The answer is to plead D2 **not** as a consequential loss flowing from the lease generally,
but as the **direct loss flowing from breach of the specific cooperation covenant** — Lease §4(b)(i) and the
no-lien/cooperation representations in Exhibit E (see Count 3 in `04-JAMS-DEMAND.md`, and evidence items E2 and E3
in `01-EVIDENCE-CHECKLIST.md`). The rate differential is the direct and foreseeable consequence of SunPower's
failure to provide the subordination/estoppel documentation the refinance required — not a downstream ripple
from the lease's existence.

**Source:** E3 (refinance file from the lender) — required before this head can be pleaded with real numbers.

## D4 — Production Guarantee shortfall (Count 5)

**Confidence label: CONTINGENT — CURRENTLY $0 ON CLAIMANT'S OWN ESTIMATE.** Guarantee Period 1 only. Guarantee
Period 2 is **not pleaded** — it is not due until approximately May 2027 (spec Count 5; see prohibitions in
`00-CASE-STRATEGY.md`/`08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`).

> **Read this before quoting any D4 figure.** D4 is **$0 on the estimates**, and that conclusion now holds on two
> independent estimates rather than one — but the margin is **thinner than previously recorded**. The Guarantee
> Period 1 threshold is **10,465 kWh** (Exhibit A, Table A). Against it:
>
> | Estimate | Source | Two-year production | Cushion |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Lease cover page, 2023-01-16 | "Estimated year 1 production 5,821" | 11,642 kWh | 1,177 kWh |
> | PURA disclosure, 2023-02-23 (E13) | "Estimated Year One Production (kWh): 5516" | **11,032 kWh** | **567 kWh** |
>
> On either figure the System **over**-produced and **there is no shortfall and no Payment Amount owed at all.** But
> a 567 kWh cushion is about 5% of two-year output — within reach of one poor irradiance year, one extended outage,
> or the **13% shading loss** the same disclosure certifies. D4 is $0 unless the E6 export shows Actual Biennial kWh
> below 10,465 kWh, and the probability that it does is **higher than the previous 1,177 kWh figure suggested**.
> Do not carry any D4 figure into a settlement valuation or a demand letter before E6 is in hand.

**What Count 5 actually claims.** The count's primary limb is not the money. It is the failure to perform the
Guarantee Period 1 biennial reconciliation and to provide the production data at all — a breach whether or not the
System under-produced. That limb survives regardless of what E6 shows, and it is the limb `04-JAMS-DEMAND.md`
Count 5 leads with. The CUTPA unfair act at Count 6 Route two (c) is likewise the failure to *reconcile*, not the
failure to remit, for the same reason.

**Formula, if and only if E6 shows a shortfall:**

```
D4 = (10,465 − Actual Biennial kWh) × $0.187     [only where Actual Biennial kWh < 10,465]
D4 = $0                                          [where Actual Biennial kWh ≥ 10,465]
```

Guarantee Period 1 threshold: 10,465 kWh at $0.187/kWh, true-up due ~May 2025 (Exhibit A §2(c), Table A).

**Sensitivity, not a claim** — the arithmetic of the formula at three points, none of which is an allegation:

| Actual Biennial kWh (from E6) | Shortfall | D4 |
|---|---|---|
| 11,642 (Lease cover-page estimate) | none — over-production of 1,177 kWh | **$0 — the money limb falls away** |
| 11,032 (installer's PURA estimate, E13) | none — over-production of 567 kWh | **$0 — the money limb falls away** |
| 10,465 (exactly at the guarantee) | 0 kWh | **$0** |
| 9,900 (arithmetic illustration only) | 565 kWh | 565 × $0.187 = $105.66 |

**Minimum Payment Amount:** Exhibit A states a **$5.00 minimum** Payment Amount threshold — a computed shortfall
payment below $5.00 is not separately payable. So even a small measured shortfall may yield nothing.

**Guarantee Period 2 is expressly not pleaded here or anywhere in this case** — it does not come due until
approximately May 2027, well after this filing.

**Source:** E6 (PVS6 monitoring export) for Actual Biennial kWh; the guarantee tables in Exhibit A for the
guaranteed figure and rate.
