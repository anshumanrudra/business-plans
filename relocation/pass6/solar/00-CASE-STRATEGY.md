# Case Strategy Index — Rudra v. SunPower Capital, LLC

NOT LEGAL ADVICE — SELF-PREPARED DRAFT. Verify all research items in the spec §14 before filing.

**This is an index, not analysis.** For the full theory of the case, count-by-count strength, statute-of-
limitations analysis, and risk register, read `2026-09-21-solar-arbitration-design.md` first. This page only
routes to the nine deliverables and states the objectives, the theory, and what not to do.

## Objectives, in priority order

1. Lease cancelled — no further payment obligation.
2. Lien removed — UCC/fixture filing terminated, land records cleared.
3. Panels kept and running — title to the System transferred to Claimant.

## Theory of the case, in three lines

- **HSSA voidness (Counts 1–2)** is the upside — if the Lease was never effective, every objective is reachable
  at once — but it is novel at the threshold and gated on the § 42-134a(a)(3) mail-or-telephone exclusion.
- **Breach of contract (Counts 3–5)** is the durable claim set — the no-lien/refinance-cooperation breach
  (Count 3) is the strongest count regardless of how Count 1 comes out.
- **§16(b) lessor default (Count 7)** is the floor — it is what stops a Count 1 threshold loss from leaving the
  Lease fully intact, by unlocking the purchase option if SunPower does not cure within 90 days of `03`.

## Do not

- **Do not stop paying.** Missing a Monthly Payment starts the §16(a)(i) default clock; §18 Early Termination
  Liability (unpaid year + FMV + removal costs) would erase every objective above. Pay under protest, without
  prejudice — see `07-FILING-LOGISTICS.md` §8.
- **Do not plead** the co-owner's non-signature, trespass, or quiet title anywhere except
  `10-SECTION-1c-DEFENCE.md`, the trigger table in `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`, and this list.
- **Do not plead the January 2023 sales conduct as a CUTPA or tort damages claim** — it is time-barred since
  January 2026; its facts support the § 42-134a(a) elements only, never a fraud or misrepresentation count.
- **Do not serve `02` alone.** Serve `02` and `03` together in Phase 0 — see the open decision gate in
  `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`.
- **Do not file `09`** absent one of the four §6.2 trigger conditions in `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`.

## §25D correction

`SOLAR-BUYOUT-RESEARCH.md` (`relocation/pass6/SOLAR-BUYOUT-RESEARCH.md`) assumed a 30% federal § 25D credit on
any buyout. That is wrong on two independent grounds, per the IRS Residential Clean Energy Credit page: "The
credit is not available for any property placed in service after December 31, 2025," and separately, "Used
(previously owned) clean energy property is not eligible." A 2026+ buyout of this System receives **no § 25D
credit** under either ground. That document has been corrected accordingly — its ITC-dependent cost tables now
show net cost equal to gross cost. Do not use its original 30%-credit figures for any settlement valuation.
