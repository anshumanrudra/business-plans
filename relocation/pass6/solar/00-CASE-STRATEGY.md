# Case Strategy Index — Rudra v. SunPower Capital, LLC

NOT LEGAL ADVICE — SELF-PREPARED DRAFT. Verify all research items in the spec §14 before filing.

**This is an index, not analysis.** For the full theory of the case, count-by-count strength, statute-of-
limitations analysis, and risk register, read `2026-09-21-solar-arbitration-design.md` first. This page only
routes to the ten deliverables and states the objectives, the theory, and what not to do.

## Objectives, in priority order

1. Lease cancelled — no further payment obligation.
2. Lien removed — UCC/fixture filing terminated, land records cleared.
3. Panels kept and running — title to the System transferred to Claimant.

## New evidence, 2026-09-23 — read this before the theory below

Four documents from Claimant's own files were added as **E13** (the Eversource/PURA interconnection and Residential
Renewable Energy Solutions packet, executed 2023-02-23, plus a revised RES application of 2023-03-07) and **E14**
(the permission-to-operate and system-energization emails of 2023-05-12 and 2023-05-15). Full detail in
`01-EVIDENCE-CHECKLIST.md`. Four consequences matter at this level:

1. **The interconnection date is settled, and it is May 2023, not April.** The April 2023 date was recollection and
   is **withdrawn**. `06` D1, `08`'s clocks and `04` ¶¶ 18–21 are corrected. The §11(a)(i) option and the §11(b)
   recapture switch now resolve in the **same week of May 2028** rather than a month apart — one decision point, not
   a window.
2. **The payment-count dispute is over.** SunPower's own written billing rule anchors the first payment to 1 June
   2023, so the count is now a function of the filing date, not of a date theory. The withdrawn "41 to 42 payments /
   $3,740.98 to $3,836.88" range must not be reinstated. `04` and `02` now carry `[COUNT]`/`[TOTAL]` placeholders to
   be filled on the filing day — `07-FILING-LOGISTICS.md` § 9 item 2 lists all four places.
3. **Count 3 gets materially stronger, which matters because it is the durable count.** The seller's agent certified
   to a state regulator, before anything was recorded, that a filing **would be recorded in the land records of
   Claimant's municipality**. That supplies the intent behind the filing and makes an "administrative slip"
   characterisation much harder. E2 is still required — the certification proves the plan, not the execution.
4. **Two new facts are deliberately being under-used, and that is the correct call.** The packet also contains a
   savings figure overstated to the regulator by roughly the whole of the Year 1 payment total, and a certification
   that the installation contract conforms to the Connecticut Home Improvement Contractor Law. Both are dated
   February 2023, **before the CUTPA boundary**, so neither can carry a damages claim. They support the § 42-134a(a)
   elements and nothing more. See the "Do not" list below and decision gate 3 in `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`.

## Theory of the case, in three lines

- **§ 42-135a unenforceability (Count 1)** is the upside and it stands on its own: if the seller failed the
  statute's notice and form requirements, the Lease has never been effective against Claimant, no cancellation is
  needed, and the January 2023 cancellation deadline is beside the point. It is novel at the threshold and gated on
  the § 42-134a(a)(3) mail-or-telephone and § 42-134a(a)(5) real-property exclusions, both of which Count 1 meets
  on the face of the pleading.
- **Cancellation (Count 2)** is secondary and alternative. It needs the § 42-137(a) window to have stayed open,
  which is an open question Claimant pleads as a contention and not as settled law; its value is the § 42-139(a)
  title-vesting route to objective 3.
- **Breach of contract (Counts 3–5)** is the durable claim set — the no-lien/refinance-cooperation breach
  (Count 3) is the strongest count regardless of how Count 1 comes out.
- **§16(b) lessor default (Count 7)** is the floor — it is what stops a Count 1 threshold loss from leaving the
  Lease fully intact, by unlocking the purchase option if SunPower does not cure within 90 days of `03`.

## Do not

- **Do not stop paying.** Missing a Monthly Payment starts the §16(a)(i) default clock; §18 Early Termination
  Liability (unpaid year + FMV + removal costs) would erase every objective above. Pay under protest, without
  prejudice — see `07-FILING-LOGISTICS.md` §8.
- **Do not plead** the co-owner's non-signature, trespass, or quiet title anywhere except
  `10-SECTION-1c-DEFENCE.md`, the trigger table in `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`, and this list.
- **Do not plead the January 2023 sales conduct as a CUTPA or tort damages claim** — it is time-barred since
  January 2026; its facts support the § 42-134a(a) elements only, never a fraud or misrepresentation count.
- **Do not plead the February 2023 PURA certifications as a CUTPA or misrepresentation claim either.** E13's
  overstated net-savings figure ($160 × 12 certified as *net*, against the Lease's own net of $72.03/month), its
  $0.314/kWh and 4% savings inputs, and its 5,516-vs-5,821 kWh production discrepancy are all dated 2023-02-23 —
  **before** the 2023-09-21 boundary `04` Count 6 pleads to. Same rule as the January 2023 conduct, same reason: they
  go to the § 42-134a(a) elements and to Count 5's reconciliation limb, never to a damages count.
- **Do not plead a Connecticut Home Improvement Act theory** anywhere until decision gate 3 in
  `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md` is resolved. Using SAVKAT's HIA-conformance certification as an *admission* supporting
  Count 1 is fine and already done; pleading § 20-429 or § 20-427(c) is not, and may be foreclosed both by
  limitations and by its tension with the § 42-134a(a)(5) real-property exclusion argument.
- **Do not treat E13's "customer need not keep paying during an extended shutdown" certification as permission to
  stop paying.** It supports Count 4 and nothing about the payment obligation. The first item on this list governs.
- **Do not serve `02` alone, and do not serve it before `04` is filed.** File `04` with JAMS first, then serve
  `02` and `03` the same day — Phase 0 in `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md` and § 1 of `07-FILING-LOGISTICS.md`. Serving
  `02` earlier opens the § 42-139(a) twenty-day window with no arbitrator appointed and no interim relief
  available, which trades objective 3 away by default.
- **Do not file `09`** absent one of the four §6.2 trigger conditions in `08-TIMELINE-AND-GATES.md`.

## §25D correction

`SOLAR-BUYOUT-RESEARCH.md` (`relocation/pass6/SOLAR-BUYOUT-RESEARCH.md`) assumed a 30% federal § 25D credit on
any buyout. That is wrong on two independent grounds, per the IRS Residential Clean Energy Credit page: "The
credit is not available for any property placed in service after December 31, 2025," and separately, "Used
(previously owned) clean energy property is not eligible." A 2026+ buyout of this System receives **no § 25D
credit** under either ground.

**E13 now closes this off from a third direction.** The Eversource Third Party Ownership Customer Disclosure assigns
both "Owner of Incentive Payments" and "Owner of Federal Investment Tax Credit" to the Primary Installer/Owner, and
not to the Customer, and the Payment Beneficiary Form designates SunPower to receive the quarterly REC Incentive
Payment. Claimant never held any federal credit or state incentive in respect of this System at any time. That also
confirms which party's credit Lease § 11(b) recaptures — SunPower's — and is worth noting for Count 7 valuation:
SunPower has been receiving quarterly REC payments from Eversource on top of the Monthly Payments.

**That document is superseded in part, and only its banner governs.** Its ITC-dependent cost tables now show net
cost equal to gross cost, and the 30%-credit assertions in its Executive Summary, its § 1 heading and bullets, and
its "Confirmed tax benefits" list have been struck and moved under a "Withdrawn" heading. Treat the rest of the
document as unverified against the actual Lease: it also guessed at the monthly payment and the escalator. Do not
use any 30%-credit figure from it for any settlement valuation, and read the banner before reading anything else in
it.
