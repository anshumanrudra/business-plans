## How to use this file

**Status keys** (put one in the `[ ]`):
- `[ ]` not started · `[~]` in progress · `[x]` done · `[!]` blocked · `[-]` skipped/N-A

**Journal convention** — after any task you touch, add a dated line beneath it:
> `— 2026-07-05: called UNHS, intl OK, $2,450/yr full-time. Waiting on transcript. Next: request TC from CT school.`

Keep entries one line, dated, and end with **Next:** so you always know the next action. Don't delete old journal lines — strike through outdated ones with `~~...~~` so history is preserved.

**Weekly ritual (10 min):** scan for `[!]` blocked items, move `[~]` forward, and add a one-line **Weekly log** entry at the very bottom.

**The 5 gating tasks** (nothing downstream moves until these are answered) are marked **🔑 GATE**. Do them first.
