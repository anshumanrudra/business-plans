## Key Takeaways for You

### IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (this week):
1. **Log into your solar billing portal** (or check recent mail) to **identify who is servicing the lease now** (post-SunPower bankruptcy). Common names to look for:
   - "SunPower SPE" (reorganized entity)
   - A new solar servicer/buyer
   - Look for the company name on your billing statement or payment portal

2. **Locate your original solar lease agreement** (likely filed with county recorder or in your home-purchase docs). Read:
   - Monthly payment amount
   - Lease term remaining
   - Transferability clause (can a tenant assume it?)
   - Buyout/early termination option
   - Maintenance obligations

3. **Schedule a system inspection** with the servicer (should be free under the lease) to **document the system is working correctly** — important because the bankruptcy transition may have created maintenance gaps.

### RENTAL DECISION:
**Recommendation:** Have **you continue paying the solar lease** (~$80-150/mo) and **adjust your asking rent up accordingly** (~$100-150/mo higher than you'd otherwise charge). This is:
- **Simplest** (no servicer approval needed, no tenant credit check)
- **A selling point** ("solar lease included — enjoy lower electric bills!")
- **Lower friction** to fill the house fast (pre-leasing before departure is critical)

**Alternative:** Have the tenant assume the lease directly — this requires:
- Servicer approval (they'll credit-check the tenant)
- Tenant willing to take on the obligation (limits your tenant pool)
- Cleaner separation (tenant pays servicer directly)

Talk to your property manager about which approach works better in your local market.

### TAX TREATMENT:
- Solar lease payments you make are **deductible as a rental expense** on Schedule E (same as utilities or HOA fees)
- Include this in your rental-income discussion with your cross-border CPA

Ask the servicer for their "change of title" or "transfer notification" form.

## Questions to Ask the Solar Servicer (when you call/email):

1. "What is the current monthly payment amount on my solar lease?"
2. "How many years/months remain on the lease term?"
3. "Is there an early buyout option? If so, what is the current buyout price?"
4. "Can the lease be transferred to a tenant? If so, what's the approval process?"
5. "What maintenance obligations do you (the servicer) have under the lease? Has the system been inspected recently?"
6. "I'm about to transfer the property title into a revocable living trust where I remain the beneficial owner — what form do I need to file to notify you?"
7. "Can I set up autopay for the monthly lease payments? What's the process?"
