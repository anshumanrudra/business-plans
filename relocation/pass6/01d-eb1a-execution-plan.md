**Timeline:**

**Consular Processing (CP) Timeline:**
1. **NVC receives case** (~2-4 weeks after approval)
2. **NVC fee payment** (~$325 per applicant + ~$120 affidavit of support) — pay online
3. **DS-260 (Immigrant Visa Application)** — complete online for each family member (you, spouse, elder son)
   - Requires: passport info, work history, addresses for last 10 years, medical exam (panel physician in India, ~$200-500 per person)
4. **Document submission** — NVC collects: birth certificates, marriage certificate, police clearances, financial docs
5. **Interview scheduling** — NVC schedules interview at **US Consulate Mumbai** (or Chennai/Hyderabad/New Delhi)
   - Wait time: **3-6 months** (no dropbox for immigrant visas; everyone interviews)
6. **Immigrant visa interview** — whole family attends; bring all docs, passports, medical exam results
   - If approved, visa stamp issued (~1 week)
7. **Enter US on immigrant visa** → become LPR (green card holder) on entry; physical green card mails to your US address in 2-4 weeks

**Your elder son's CSPA age is frozen at the "visa availability" date** (= I-140 approval date, since EB-1 India is current). If approved ~Oct 2027, he's ~14 → safely under 21 when he seeks to acquire (within 1 year of visa availability per CSPA rules).

**Total timeline: File Sep 2027 → Enter US as LPR by ~Jun 2028** (9 months, assuming no RFE)

## Handling RFEs (Request for Evidence)

RFEs are **common** on EB-1A (USCIS is strict on the "extraordinary ability" bar). Typical requests:

1. **"Provide more evidence that your contributions are of *major significance* to the field"**
   - Response: Additional recommendation letters (recruit 2-3 more experts), more detailed impact metrics (adoption data, citations, awards), external validation (press coverage, industry recognition)

2. **"Demonstrate sustained acclaim, not just peer recognition"**
   - Response: Media coverage (#3), awards (#1), speaking invitations (conferences, panels), evidence of international reach (global users, international citations)

3. **"Clarify how your role is *leading or critical*"**
   - Response: More detailed org chart, testimonials from Disney leadership, evidence that Disney is "distinguished" (Fortune 500 ranking, market cap, industry awards)

4. **"Letters are too generic; provide more specific evidence"**
   - Response: Revise letters to include concrete examples, metrics, comparisons to peers; get letters from more senior/recognized experts

**RFE response timeline:**
- USCIS gives **84 days (12 weeks)** to respond
- You can submit the response earlier (no need to wait the full 84 days)
- **Premium Processing may be available for the response** (speeds up adjudication after you submit)

**Strategy:**
- Work with your attorney to analyze the RFE; don't panic (RFEs are normal)
- Respond comprehensively (over-respond rather than under-respond)
- If the RFE asks for "more evidence of X," provide 2-3 additional pieces (not just 1)

**If denied after RFE:**
- You can **refile immediately** (no waiting period for EB-1A)
- OR **file a motion to reopen/reconsider** (argues USCIS made a legal error)
- OR **appeal to the Administrative Appeals Office (AAO)** (takes 6-12 months, low success rate)
- **Refiling is usually faster** than appealing

## What If EB-1A Fails? (The High-Stakes Scenario)

**If EB-1A is denied** (on first submission or after RFE):
1. **Refile immediately** — address the denial reasons; strengthen weak criteria; get more/better recommendation letters
2. **OR pivot to O-1A** for return + try EB-1A again from the US (but the clock keeps ticking on elder son's CSPA age)
3. **OR accept that elder son ages out** — he'd need his own visa as an adult (H-1B lottery, F-1 student, etc.) and can't ride your green card as a derivative

**The brutal reality:** If EB-1A fails and Disney won't sponsor EB-1C for ≥2 years, **there is no employer backup** and **no Canada fallback** (per your decision). Your elder son's durable US status depends **entirely** on EB-1A approval.

**Mitigation:**
- **Execute the evidence roadmap aggressively** (no shortcuts; target 4-6 criteria with strong quality)
- **Engage a top EB-1A attorney** (not a generalist; someone with proven EB-1A success)
- **File on time** (~Sep 2027) with a comprehensive petition (don't rush at the last minute)
- **If you get an RFE, respond comprehensively** (over-respond; don't under-deliver)
